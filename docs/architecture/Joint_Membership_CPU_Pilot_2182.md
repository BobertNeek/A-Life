# Joint-membership CPU research: cost-matched pilot

This is a negative research result, not a new default policy or a promoted brain.
The opt-in CPU consumer compares applying joint-observed family recall against
withholding it while retaining the same query, event capture and work-accounting
rules. Neither condition invents action-isolated physiology or a second learner.
Ordinary target recall and the existing context cap remain unchanged.

## Matched evidence

Twelve fresh lives used one full cumulative asset/genome pair, two sampling seeds,
three scenes, 600 real preparation intervals and a 96-interval observation horizon.
Both arms survived all six lives and consumed all four food opportunities.

| Scene | Withheld meal interval | Applied meal interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hazard choice, pair 1 | 68 | 70 |
| Hazard choice, pair 2 | 49 | 49 |
| Moving Food, pair 1 | 19 | 19 |
| Moving Food, pair 2 | 21 | 22 |

Failed toy Ingest executions were 32 in each condition. In the second hazard
pair, final injury rose from 0.18823 withheld to 0.25583 applied. Injury at the
shared meal interval49 was effectively equal (~0.0670152); the extra damage
occurred during subsequent hazard interactions, not food acquisition. There is no
measured overall advantage and no recommendation to enable the route by default.

Birth and prepared resident states, actor geometry, initial energy and initial
logits matched across every pair. First-query sources and all query/admission
work fields also matched. Sampled choices later diverged in five of six pairs.
This establishes real decision influence under the matched protocol, not improved
intelligence or isolated causal credit. Realized work may diverge after behavior.

The sensor profile is omnidirectional GroundedObjectSlotsV1: moving Food is not
live prey, and these are not gaze-search or occlusion tests. Exact-target recall
is not evidence of generalization to a newly identified food object. Two seed
pairs are a small pilot. Player feedback was not newly qualified in this pilot.

## Qualification and preservation

Focused qualification passed 92 Core tests, six native membership tests, five
legacy native tests and 25 local assay tests; two explicitly external/ignored
fixtures were not run. Strict focused Clippy and formatting passed. Tests include
cold continuation, changed-mode rejection, queried-versus-applied provenance,
transaction rollback and insufficient-budget failure. No GPU or ordinary PPO
admission is claimed; enabled research rows are rejected by ordinary collectors.

All 36 birth/prepared/final checkpoints and 36 founder/Adam records were retained
locally and hashed. Founder/Adam bytes remained unchanged within each life. Raw
creature state, neural arrays and the separate local assay are not published here.
