# Evolutionary Intelligence Roadmap Overview

The full program is defined in `EVOLUTIONARY_INTELLIGENCE_ROADMAP.md`.

## V0: Visual and player-experience overhaul

- Replace the prototype presentation with the approved player-facing direction.
- Make actions, needs, speech, relationships, and learning readable.
- Preserve the GPU-authoritative neural boundary.

## EI0: Evolution laboratory

- Activate diploid DNA, chemistry, development, and causal phenotype expression.
- Add wild, reserve, managed, and school habitats.
- Add pedigrees, selection tools, test arenas, provenance, and population budgets.

## EI1: Norn-plus early mammal

- Evolve flexible survival, spatial memory, reversal, transfer, imitation, and
  grounded starter speech.
- Target lab-rat-level adaptive behavior in simplified worlds.

## EI2: Social mammal and proto-pack

- Select individual and group intelligence.
- Develop transferable social learning, roles, leadership, trust, and culture.

## EI3: Pack intelligence

- Develop flexible coordinated hunting, defense, sharing, and group planning.

## EI4: Hunter-gatherer proto-culture

- Develop tools, settlements, division of labor, norms, teaching, and persistent
  material culture.

## EI5: Abstract developmental generalist

- Add staged schooling, writing, mathematics, science, planning, self-report,
  and cross-domain transfer.

## EI6: Individual ascension

- Migrate one exceptional creature through proven larger brain classes.
- Preserve identity while new capacity develops through gated education.

## EI7: Advanced evaluation

- Compare continual learning, reasoning, social cognition, and transfer with
  suitable conventional models under recorded conditions.

## EI8: Commercial and compute flywheel

- Ship the game if it is fun and stable.
- Use revenue to fund larger validated experiments without making cloud compute
  mandatory for ordinary play.

No stage starts because a date arrived. Each starts only after its preceding
behavioral, hardware, save, soak, and provenance gate passes.
