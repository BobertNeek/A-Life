# V19: preserve genuine meals while keeping reflex outcomes separate

This is an explicit CPU/world research component, not a default-game activation or learned behavior claim. It continues the protected [V18 component](../awake-shared-cpu-v18/PUBLICATION.md). The original 54 manifest-bound payloads and their SHA256SUMS are retained byte-for-byte in this directory.

## What changed and why

V18 correctly withheld voluntary-action learning when a withdrawal changed execution, but also discarded real meal evidence when a late response had literally no time left. V19 permits ordinary sealed credit only when the response has exactly zero time, effort and displacement, no candidate-altered voluntary execution, and the exact ordinary normalized body event. Otherwise the host consumes dispatch authority without inventing a voluntary experience, replay row, predictor observation or selected-action eligibility increment. Historical eligibility decay is retained.

A reproduced terrain case exposed a subtler flaw: candidate-only prefix rejection could change movement before a later zero-time response. A monotonic alteration flag now records preemption, clipping or rejection even when no withdrawal receipt exists. It cannot be cleared by a later apparently harmless response.

## Unresolved issues, not hidden by the passing control

At body X=3, reconstructing elapsed time from rounded absolute-coordinate displacement produced a false residual interval of about 9.54e-7 and nonzero effort. The origin-centered fixture isolates the exact-zero branch; it does not fix this translated-coordinate defect. A separate explicit-duration revision is required. The exact equivalence predicate was not loosened.

Curved-ground prefix rejection can still block movement that ordinary execution permits. The alteration flag prevents false learning, but the trajectory needs a separate physical correction. Positive-time intervention still conservatively withholds whole-tick episodic credit, including genuinely completed unaffected actions. GPU/live-game integration and full learning qualification are absent.
