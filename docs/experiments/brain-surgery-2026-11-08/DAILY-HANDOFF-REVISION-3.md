## Keep the original research in view

## Natural memory engagement, now measured on actual policy states

The completed [passive reader-v3 probe](reader-v3/FINDINGS.md) used two fresh OFF-policy lives, each with 600 world-only preparation ticks and 256 primary steps. Each ate one harmful meal and 14 safe meals, with no safeguard stop. All 512 same-state baseline replays matched the actual selected bundle, every shadow preserved state bytes, and native score decomposition/recurrent equality checks passed. Only the unchanged OFF brain acted.

The operation-memory port was nonzero in 346 of 349 harmful Close candidates, all 346 tied to exact consumed Ingest evidence. Some Release inputs came from nonconsumption Ingest records; provenance is not silently generalized. A gain of 1/16 changed nine instantaneous bundles across 512 states, with maximum logit shifts about .134 and .132. Larger gains altered more choices and the largest clipped a candidate. Existing target-memory routing and small acquired operation offsets were already active.

Those nine changes are not nine improvements. None selects safe Eat. Five change zero-port representatives through normalization/arbitration; others omit Close, add Release, or substitute Play that can itself prepare a grip. The [complete flip audit](reader-v3/FLIP-AUDIT.md) retains possible feeding-supporting directions and useful-handling/rest risks without inventing alternate outcomes. A separately frozen actual OFF-versus-low-gain comparison is the next question; no later allocation is part of the closed v3 result.

## All-drive review: a real saturation defect, not an all-clear

The complete eleven-lane [matrix](drive-audit-v1/drive-matrix.md) and [five-arm result table](drive-audit-v1/results.md) are retained with [source and corrections](drive-audit-v1/ARCHIVE.md). Drives enter encoder neurons in order 0,3,7,1,2,4,5,6,8,9,10; array position is not drive identity.

The first 64 motor neurons have inherited dendritic branches with gain 6. In controlled native receptor contexts these branches nearly saturate the tanh operating point. A dedicated fresh-founder recipe lowers only birth gain to 0.5, with a distinct foundation/compatibility identity. It does not rewrite acquired dendrites on restore. Sixty-nine focused identity, legacy-byte, CPU continuation and GPU-upload/Naga guards passed for that new recipe; actual GPU execution remains untested.

Over eight fixed native-context frames, encoded fatigue→Rest logit sensitivity rises from 0.001401 to 0.023249, hunger→Approach from 0.001944 to 0.032257, and curiosity→Inspect from 0.000398 to 0.006610. These are measured neural responses with innate contributions held fixed, not learned behavior or demonstrated rest choice. Lower gain changes reference-neuron signs too; an independently labeled Approach/Inspect reference-weight revision repairs the resulting sweet-approach polarity.

Five matched arms each ran 17 controlled scenarios for eight ordinary policy/world steps (680 total policy steps). The reference-recalibrated candidate preserved close and held sweet feeding, ate legal max-reach food in a case baseline missed, and avoided eating bitter food under seeded pain where baseline ate. However, those bitter objects were nutritious and had hazard_pain=0: this is not causal injury avoidance or learned harm recall.

Two attempted distance/taste revisions were rejected. Both lost legal max-reach feeding and neither repaired tired-state Rest selection; weakening taste also reintroduced the bitter-plus-pain consumption. The nominal coefficients in historical notes precede the inherited 0.25 ActionCandidate scale: actual signal-row taste/distance coefficients are 3/−1.5 (baseline), 0.6/−4 (soft geometry), and 3/−20 (distance only). Complete logits additionally depend on motor activation, reference contributions and other native terms.

These fixtures use GroundedObjectSlotsV1, which exposes distant target chemistry. The contact-gated GroundedTerrainVisionV1 path is not qualified by those distant sweet/bitter results. The original safe summary retains only broad ActionKind; the separately versioned actions-v2 projection adds exact selected IDs/families and recorded physical outcomes. Unrecorded primitive, per-channel success and persistent per-tick consumed state remain explicitly unknown.

Loneliness, ATP, temperature, reproduction and boredom have zero encoded action effect in this particular frozen weight set. That does not mean no bodily effect: ATP changes compute affordability, chemistry and innate responses remain, and boredom can accumulate and be relieved by actual play. It does mean we cannot call all motivations correctly wired. Social, thermal, reproductive and boredom-driven behavior remain unresolved. Temperature stress is unsigned; generic avoidance does not establish choosing a warmer or cooler refuge. Newborn isolated fixtures cannot qualify mature social/reproductive behavior.

## Recovery must be real

A separate inherited [waking/recovery calibration](wake-calibration-v1/FINDINGS.md) addresses the old recipe's timescale: its controlled full-expression waking pressure remains below normal entry threshold even at the expressed 4,514-tick lifespan. An explicit gene recipe (.001 waking gain, .01 recovery rather than a near-total one-event reset) passed 28 native core tests, including normal pressure entry in a developing body. Adequate reserve is deliberately controlled in that test; it is not a self-feeding lifetime or completed consolidation cycle. Defaults and loaded concentrations remain untouched.

The independent [world-only Rest/motion correction](rest-motion-v1/FINDINGS.md) has stronger direct regression evidence: before repair, actual movement plus a simultaneous Rest channel received full sleep recovery. The narrow fix covers translation only: it suppresses sleep recovery when any realized translational path segment moves, including cancelling out-and-back segments. Yaw-only body turning plus Rest remains unresolved; actual body-yaw change, rather than requested turn or effort alone, needs a separate tested factor. Blocked or stationary Rest remains valid. Four focused tests and 585 full-world tests passed (three existing manual tests ignored), preserving effort, doses, rollback and one biology advance. This source is being reconciled onto current main for separate publication; it is not silently bundled with the chemistry candidate.

## Existing motor planning and the pain shortcut

The brain already has a 224-neuron ActionPlanning lobe, but its inherited lateral inhibition covers only six tiles and does not implement comprehensive competition across eight action families. Independent channel selection can also choose locomotion toward one target and manipulation toward another. Those are real architectural constraints; adding a new lobe by name would not resolve target binding or prove better arbitration. Global winner-take-all is not automatically desirable either: looking while eating is useful, while moving and sleeping are physically inconsistent.

The isolated directional-withdrawal kernel passed all 592 native world tests, including eleven new localized contact, heading fallback, collision/carry, rollback, one-biology and save→next-step fixtures. It is an explicit prototype API, not ordinary gameplay promotion. Credible external contact supplies an away normal. Acute unlocalized pain instead produces the user's requested brief backward translation along current heading, with yaw preserved. Chronic injury alone does not latch movement; opposed contacts do not invent a direction.

Sealed CPU integration is still in progress. Involuntary displacement must be distinguished from the selected command in versioned learning evidence, with one credit/biology transaction and legacy wire boundaries preserved. A further source defect was found in the sleeping/no-command tick path: it detects stationary hazard contact but discards the damage. That correction and pain-triggered sleep interruption are separate, unqualified factors. The sleep controller currently assumes completed consolidation before normal waking; an emergency interruption must not fake a completed cycle or discard pending replay.

## Continue with evidence, not constant activity

Competing bodily needs can produce sustained changing motivation only when their accumulation, relief, perception, neural paths and actual affordances form working loops. They are not emotion merely because concentrations conflict. Rest, satiation and quiet behavior remain valid outcomes. Next work is to finish the missing pathways and honest sleep/reflex integration, test actual edited-policy trajectories, retain failed variants, and distinguish world-rule benefits from neural and biochemical changes. No candidate in this handoff is promoted to accepted lives.
