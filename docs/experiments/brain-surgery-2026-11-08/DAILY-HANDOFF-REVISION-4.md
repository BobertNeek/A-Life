# Brain surgery, revision 4: reject an engaged but unhelpful prior

This is an additive chronological handoff. Earlier candidates, failed attempts,
the exhausted eight-case study and the historical build-provenance qualification
remain unchanged. None of these experiments replaces an accepted creature.

## Research method

[Sparks of Weight-Space Programming, MIT CSAIL / OASYS](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)
remains the central reference. Experiment 4 motivates targeted repair,
Experiment 5 constructs whole 20,486-weight static game networks, and
Experiment 6 uses measured feedback to revise a candidate. Our recurrent,
embodied and plastic system is a different setting. Handwritten inherited
weights, acquired episodic recall, lifetime synaptic plasticity and optimizer
updates are different mechanisms. A changed logit is not a useful learned skill.

## Actual low-gain comparison: no feeding or safety benefit

The [completed v5 comparison](reader-v5/FINDINGS.md) ran two fresh sampling
identities, each once with the interface-only brain and once with a gain-1/16
cross-verb memory reader. That is four lives of one fixed recipe plus its edit,
not four independent genetic or trained-brain replications. Both arms share the
explicit-grip graph and the Rest-translation-corrected world. Exactly 16 inherited
operation-port coefficients differ. No newer stationary-contact dose, reflex,
boredom or biochemical candidate was mixed into the comparison.

Every life completed 600 world-only preparation ticks and 256 ordinary policy
steps. Both paired preparation barriers passed. The native replay, score
decomposition and state-immutability gates passed on all 1,024 decisions. No
teacher actions, forced sleep, replacement seeds, adaptive extension or retry was
used. Preparation is not neural experience.

Every arm ate 13 safe meals and one harmful meal. Grip-transfer counts matched
within both pairs. The low-gain reader changed one joint choice in the first
pair and 24 in the second, but produced no feeding or safety improvement. Final
energy differences were tiny and opposite in sign: approximately -0.00001019
and +0.00000942. Five fewer failed operations in the second pair is a real
secondary difference, not evidence of general self-care improvement.

The reader did engage naturally. Its H/Close and H/Release inputs mostly came
from exact consumed Ingest evidence, but some came from other Ingest-family
events. All provenance is reported rather than relabeled as a meal. At the first
changed second-pair decision, OFF closed on H while ON tried H/Play and failed;
safe food was inactive. Delaying harmless handling is not safer eating. In the
first pair, changed normalization switched a zero-port Rest representative to a
zero-port failed Vocalize without directly routing memory into either verb.

**Decision: do not adopt or amplify this inherited cross-verb prior merely to
force visible behavior.** Retain the explicit grip, heldness and operation-memory
interface. Harmful food is harmful to consume, not intrinsically to hold or
transport. The simpler atomic-swap world repair already removed the practical
held-A / selected-Eat(B) obstruction. The next useful hypothesis is
[operation-specific physical efficacy and plan feasibility](reader-v5/NEXT-HYPOTHESIS.md),
not stronger generic aversion to H. Exact-Play/boredom work is a separate factor.

## Directional pain and motivation remain active work

The isolated body-only pain candidate now passes 611 world tests and 30 CPU
fixtures, with three existing world and one existing CPU manual tests ignored.
An actual low-ATP sleeping resident withdrew physically without inventing a
decision or credit, while neural checkpoint, sequence and completed-cycle count
were preserved. Exact restored continuation matched. Acute unlocalized pain
uses the requested short backward-heading movement; credible external contact
uses its away direction. Chronic injury alone does not latch a new reflex.

This remains opt-in development, not ordinary GPU gameplay qualification.
Honest cognitive interruption must retain pending replay and consolidation:
new neural writes cannot race a frozen transaction whose later commit would
overwrite them. The actual GPU world producer and credit path are being wired
and tested separately. A software-Vulkan baseline diagnostic matched original
and candidate across 7,947 cases, but the existing pinned golden test failed
first on a header-corruption case. Same-adapter equivalence is not a passing
pinned-golden, hardware-parity or gameplay claim.

The all-drive audit is still not an all-clear. Loneliness, thermal direction,
reproduction and balanced fatigue/rest policy need further grounding and actual
behavior tests. Boredom is being tested as a complete accumulation-to-action-to-
relief loop, with truly matched failed-Play controls and rest/feeding competition.
Earlier excessive-tonic and oversuppressed variants were rejected. Its current
fixed schedule is underway, not a completed result in this handoff. We should
allow satiation, rest and quiet behavior rather than engineer constant activity
and call it emotion.
