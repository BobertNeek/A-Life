remains the main reference: experiment 4 motivates targeted repair, experiment 5

weight vector. We trace perception, neural dynamics, action, real physical
consequence and acquired recall separately, then revise against measured failure.

stream; removing only the newly added consequence reader produced 7 and 4

are exact-action episodic effects, not an Adam update or generic Other reward.

## All drives: no premature all-clear

The ordinary new-game path defaults to Nano512; explicit research selections
can choose N2048. That source fact does not prove which launcher or retained
creature the user is currently running.

Fresh Nano inventory found every drive lane encoded, but many have no compiled
recurrent route to an actual nonzero decoder row. The current scaffold sends
Interoception to Reserve without an outgoing macro route. However, static graph
traversal alone is incomplete: an existing motor birth dendrite crosses a lobe

retained. Acquired structural cross-edges can also bridge regions in older lives;
fresh-founder results do not prove learned lives are disconnected.

social learning is impossible. Matched peer/toy flat observations also expose an

zero-bridge control. Its first eight preservation fixtures passed. A later repeated-state fixture
now carries all 11 lanes into planning, but unchanged output weights make
