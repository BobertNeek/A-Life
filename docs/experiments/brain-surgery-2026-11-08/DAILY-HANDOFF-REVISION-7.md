The independent source audit found no simple inversion in the current valuation
signs. Homeostatic deltas are after−before. Relief of hunger/fatigue/fear/pain/
loneliness/temperature has negative delta with −1/7 weight; ATP and body energy
have positive deltas with +1/7. Curiosity/reproduction/reserved have zero generic
relief weight. Positive pain is excluded from the relief sum, then fresh injury
uses max(pain rise, injury rise, health loss,0), avoiding that double charge.
Boredom's drive Extension0 is not endocrine Extension0 (praise).

Innate scores are not sums over all conflicting pressures. They use the maximum
masked raw drive above .55, family gain, cue and reach. Current gains are Approach
4.4544, Ingest 6.6816, Avoid 7.1424 and Rest 3.84; corresponding theoretical cue-one
maxima are 2.00448, 3.00672, 3.21408 and 1.728. These are contributions, not final
policy logits. Arbitrarily summing them across families would double-count common
pressure and can force perpetual action instead of preserving rest and satiation.

All eleven drives also enter acquired-memory query coordinates 12..23. Recomputing
recall can change confidence and values even without a direct motor route. Fixed-
recall detached probes intentionally exclude that influence. Normal GPU selection
has curiosity-scaled novelty/gap exploration suppressed by urgency; the CPU PPO
masked softmax used in these experiments does not include that selector term.
This limits gameplay transfer and is not, by itself, a new parity bug.

Current target-memory need latents pool outcomes across actions; family memory
contains aggregate valence/success/danger/energy. Neither is a historical per-need,
per-action causal reward table. Do not reinterpret saved coordinates or route a
pooled target relief value indiscriminately into every action family.
