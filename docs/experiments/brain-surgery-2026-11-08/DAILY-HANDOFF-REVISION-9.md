already have the paper's capabilities. Experiment 4 selectively repaired a trained
control network; experiment 5 constructed whole 20,486-weight game networks;

attempts use that inspect, hypothesize, edit, measure and revise approach.

matched ablations, exact source identities and the distinction between static
weight design, acquired episodic evidence and synaptic learning. Do not overwrite
trained weights, optimizer moments/ages, replay, recovery journals or checkpoints.

of old coordinates. More activity is not the goal: rest and satiation must remain
lawful outcomes.

## A genuine native arithmetic milestone, still not a living policy

The new detached reference uses eleven separate signed consequences and current
biological readings. Paired branches share a monotone dendritic basis and compare
current pressure with a bounded hypothetical successor. Hunger, fatigue, fear,
pain, loneliness and temperature stress have burden semantics. ATP is availability
relative to an explicit proposed .75 target. Curiosity, reproductive arousal and
untyped extensions do not receive invented deficit valence; boredom requires its
explicit species binding. Equal reference magnitudes and gain .35 are engineered
priors, not fitted biological constants.

frozen scalar-reference tests. Fourteen sealed synthetic-source evaluations and

absolute error 0, against tolerance 2e-7. Fifteen existing sparse-projection and

and dendritic conjunctions: five projections, 112 fixed edges over ten active
