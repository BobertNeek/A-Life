# Hunger-path variant

The opt-in fresh experimental variant in `bold-hunger-gain.patch` increases three existing hunger-path recurrent weights on top of the combined candidate; it is not enabled by default. In the same two-seed, 120-decision low-energy trials, outcome counts remained unchanged: neutral food consumed 1/2, palatable food 2/2, bitter food 0/2. In the neutral case that was not consumed, the final Ingest candidate logit changed from about -0.658 to -0.195; the corresponding motor activation shifted from 0.278 to -0.956. These observations show a substantial internal change but no demonstrated behavioral benefit in this pilot. Do not promote this extra gain on this evidence; keep the previously tested combined candidate unchanged. This is a disposable CPU founder experiment, not gameplay or GPU validation.
