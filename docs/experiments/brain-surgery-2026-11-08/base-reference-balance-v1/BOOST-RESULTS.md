# Stronger Ingest-reference weight comparison

Native CPU research only. Source base 205f60c200dc05f4f0ae8c02d3f5b9747dbc0abd plus `ingest-boost.patch`; dev executable built successfully. Relative to whole-care, the boost edits only two Ingest decoder weights: 26927 30→37.5, and 27135 -60→-75. No user checkpoint, default founder or retained life was changed. No claim of GPU gameplay or learned competence is made.

Prospective comparison completed 24 trials: four fresh matched seeds 2026111510–1513, nearby positive food, distant positive food, and harmless bitter food, two arms, 32 steps. Both ate nearby food 4/4; the boosted arm ate distant food 2/4 while the original ate 0/4; both left bitter food uneaten 4/4 within the bound. Every trial reported exact cold-next-step continuation and unchanged founder optimizer. These small paired observations suggest a candidate worth more testing, not a reliable general improvement. Failure to consume bitter food is not a learned aversion or general welfare result; nutrition and risk vary in a real world. Independent trajectories diverge after sampled choices. No default or accepted policy is promoted on these data.
