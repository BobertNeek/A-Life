# Outcome-to-eligibility trace: two diagnostic repeats

These are exact diagnostic reruns of already-used seed 2026111551, red-safe/red-left, safe-first and harm-first, with normal fast writes. Both repeated the frozen matrix's actual four exposure histories and all sampled probe actions, physical targets and contacts exactly; choices and cold-next continuation matched. Observers logged the real sealed outcome and receptor frame and before/after fast/eligibility for Ingest and Approach decoder color lanes; no host state or action was changed. This is not a new seed or an independent learning result.

In safe-first, immediately before/after the first harmful blue-food outcome (pain 0.240), the resulting mean Ingest eligibility was -0.287 on the prior safe red lane and -0.384 on the harmful blue lane. The negative receptor factor averaged -0.0602 for both; summed fast deltas were +0.000353 on red and +0.000471 on blue. Approach eligibilities were 0.950 red and 0.888 blue with summed fast deltas -0.001167/-0.001090. Thus the measured pain-modulated update was not confined to the current harmful cue: a substantial prior-cue trace was still present. In harm-first, at the first harmful blue exposure, mean Ingest red/blue eligibilities were -0.0305/-0.2745, consistent with less prior red carryover in that first event. All findings are conditional on this one seed and short exposure spacing.

Negative motor activations and decoder weights mean a positive fast-weight delta does not by itself imply a positive action-logit effect. The prior cue matrix found a tiny, order-dependent post-exposure score difference and identical normal/sham choices; these traces support cross-exposure credit contamination as a plausible contributor, not a proof it is the sole cause. Receptor-derived appetitive/aversive lanes 6/7 were zero in these observed newborn frames, while measured pain was present in the direct pain lane; do not claim there was no pain signal. Simply amplifying all learning could amplify the wrong association. Test targeted timing/eligibility interventions against both immediate and delayed-credit controls before changing the default. No production code, user's saved creature or GPU path was modified.
