# Why the two-food pilot oscillated

The diagnostic reran the same seed 2026111546 with only detached logging added. All four training exposures and all 64 recorded sampled actions, physical targets and contacts matched the prior trace exactly; neither food was consumed in the probe. This is a reproduction, not an additional test of learning.

On the first probe decision, Approach scores for the two foods were 18.553/18.584, while their Ingest scores were 13.744/13.748. The largest native action-decoder contribution to Approach was +19.196 from primary chemical lane 15; distance contributed -0.551. Ingest's lane 15 contributed +14.242 and distance -0.409. Action-decoder lane sums did not exactly equal final logits; residuals on these candidates were approximately -0.059 to -0.090, so lane terms are not the entire scoring path. At decision 16, touching one food lowered that target's Approach via the contact term by about 11.287, but the other uncontacted food retained an 18.525 Approach score, above the touched food's 13.923 Ingest score. This supports an action-competition explanation for this particular oscillation; it is not a general diagnosis or evidence of preference.

The next candidate will reduce the existing Approach taste-readout contribution while checking both the two-food task and ordinary distant-food seeking.
