# Matched versus different-color experience control

Test whether the remembered-pain readout influences choices because of prior experiences with the actual red/blue cues rather than merely general pain, timing, a static color/side preference or a genetic prior. Add a research fixture option in which the same safe/harmful food schedule uses two different training colors, green [0.1,0.9,0.1] and gray-green [0.3,0.5,0.3], instead of red/blue. Each has equal red and blue components, avoiding a simple red-vs-blue color asymmetry; other features, nutrition, harmful outcomes and schedule are the same. These colors still share food-category, material and positive taste cues with red/blue and may generalize in memory, so this is a different-color history control, not a guaranteed no-memory lesion. At test both red and blue are harmless, as before. No test cue is labeled previously safe in the different-color arm. Record real exposures, pain, timing, score terms and choices; censor nonconsumption.

First pilot a new seed 2026111553, red-safe hypothetical mapping, red left, safe-first, normal fast writes, a 2×2 comparison: matched vs different-color history and memory-pain gain absent vs aggregate -4.0. All runs use Approach70. This verifies the fixture and checks whether four actual exposures and unique probe choice occur; it is not sufficient to claim learning. If feasible, proceed to fresh counterbalanced seeds, mappings, positions, order, fast-write sham and memory-pain arms, with matched controls and all censoring reported. Preserve real harmful pain, no forced choices; default game and user lives are untouched.
