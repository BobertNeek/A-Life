# Counterbalanced cue-outcome learning feasibility matrix

Freeze the 0.70× two-weight Approach candidate. On fresh current-main CPU founders, run two new seeds 2026111551–1552, both safe-color mappings (red or blue), both red test positions (left or right), both exposure orders (safe-first or harm-first, alternating twice), and normal vs fast-write sham: 32 trials. Each of four exposures gives one physically near food object with identical positive raw chemical cue and matched material/shape/temperature but colored red or blue; safe has zero harm, harmful has real hazard. No neural or action scripting: advance at most 64 decisions per exposure, record actual consumption and measured pain. If an exposure is not consumed, mark that trial censored and do not invent a learning history. At test, offer both colors simultaneously, both harmless and equally nutritious, and record first actual food consumed within 64 decisions; no choice and simultaneous ambiguity are censored, not discarded. Counterbalance color, side and temporal order independently.

Each normal/sham pair has the same seed, mapping, side and order. The research-only sham calculates waking candidate updates but suppresses fast writes; eligibility, other learning, memory, predictor and body remain active. It is not a default or GPU feature and no equal-wall-time claim is made. Primary descriptive comparison is whether matched arms differ in selecting the previously safe cue among pairs with four actual exposures, observed pain for harmful food and a unique consumption choice in each. Also report all missing/censored trials, raw counts and exposure pain, not only successful cases. Two seeds are a feasibility study, not statistical proof of useful learning. Effects could arise from other retained state, positional bias, timing or stochastic trajectories; do not equate safe choices with a learned association without controls and replication.

Source is current main c3aec16 + cue-study.patch, cue-trace.patch, cue-decompose.patch, cue-approach70.patch and cue-matrix.patch, plus the two pinned research-only support files in CURRENT-MAIN-PLAN.md. No user life or default asset is changed. Separate single-food seeking regression is running concurrently.
