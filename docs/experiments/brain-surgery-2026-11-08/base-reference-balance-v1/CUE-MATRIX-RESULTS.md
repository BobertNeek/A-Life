# Counterbalanced real cue-outcome test: no demonstrated useful fast learning

Current-main c3aec16 experimental fresh founders with the frozen two-weight Approach70 candidate. The preregistered 32 CPU trials completed: two seeds 2026111551–1552 × safe color red/blue × red test side left/right × safe-first/harm-first × normal/sham. The sixteen normal/sham pairs share seed and condition; rows using the same seed are correlated, not 32 independent creatures in a statistical sample. All four real exposure foods were consumed in each trial. Safe exposure pain was zero, harmful exposures produced pain approximately 0.240 and 0.232. Each probe offered both colors as harmless equally nutritious food, and each trial made a unique physical consumption choice. No action was forced. All 32 exact cold-next-step and unchanged-founder-optimizer checks passed. Normal fast entries changed (25,760 for seed 1551, 25,880 for 1552); sham retained zero fast changes.

Each mode chose the previously safe color in 4/16 setups and the previously harmful color in 12/16. Most importantly, the normal and sham arms chose the exact same color in all 16 matched setups. This is a negative result for useful fast-write learning in this bounded task, not proof no learning is possible generally. The foods in the probe were harmless regardless of training history. Other memory, predictor, physiology and state evolution remained active in both arms, and no equal-wall-time claim is made.

A post-hoc diagnostic of the first test-decision Ingest logits found sham red/blue differences exactly zero. In normal mode, safe-minus-harmful scores were about +0.00160 or +0.00062 when safe exposure came first, but about -0.00030 or -0.00034 when harm came first. Those are tiny order-dependent score differences, not established preference, not a predeclared significance test, and not consistent in the direction of useful safe selection. The actual choices did not differ between matched arms. Do not simply amplify this signal without understanding why the sign depends on order.

`cue-matrix-summary.json` includes all 32 cue mappings, exposure and pain evidence, choices, fast counts, score differences and hashes of the full local traces. No user life, trained checkpoint or default game asset was modified. Next investigate the outcome-to-eligibility credit path and reward timing, not merely claim that the presence of plastic weight changes constitutes learning. Separately evaluate the Approach70 candidate on independent food-seeking regressions; it remains unpromoted.
