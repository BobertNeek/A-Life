# Fresh counterbalanced cue-history comparison

Evaluate the frozen Approach70 founder with the optional authored Ingest remembered-pain readout (aggregate -4.0 in the 48 previously zero MemoryContext lane-26 weights) versus no such readout. Two fresh seeds 2026111554–1555 × safe-color mapping red/blue × red test side left/right × safe-first/harm-first order × matched red/blue vs different-color training history × authored-readout off/on × waking fast-write normal/sham = 128 CPU trials. This is an initial feasibility block, not an endpoint if useful learning or a meaningful failure warrants follow-up. Cells sharing a seed and sequence are correlated; do not count them as 128 independent organisms for statistical claims.

Every trial must use four actual unforced food exposures (two safe, two harmful), verify consumption and record actual pain. In matched histories the physical cue colors in training are the red and blue later offered; in different-color histories they are green and gray-green, both symmetric in red/blue components, while the safe/harmful schedule and non-color properties are matched. Different-color histories can generalize by food category and physical similarity, so they are not a no-memory lesion. Both test foods are harmless and equally nutritious, and in the different-color histories neither probe cue has a real prior safe identity. Record first actual consumption, no-choice/censored cases, exact edits, target cue scores and memory readouts, physical outcomes, fast state counts, cold-next/optimizer checks, all failures and per-trial hashes. No forced action, fabricated pain or silent exclusion.

Primary descriptive comparisons: within the matched-history conditions, compare which previously safe/harmful cue is actually eaten with readout on vs off, paired on seed/mapping/side/order/fast mode; compare the same raw red/blue choices under matched vs different-color histories; and compare normal vs fast-write-sham, which still retains episodic memory and other learning. Differences in sampled trajectories or bodily states are possible and must be reported. A safe choice alone is not proof of synaptic learning; any cue-specific effect must survive the alternative-history and bias controls. This uses one experimental authored fresh founder family, no GPU gameplay, no default replacement, no user-saved life, and no promoted or production research flags. If a result is promising, continue with additional independent seeds and delayed/reversal/unrelated-pain safety tests.
