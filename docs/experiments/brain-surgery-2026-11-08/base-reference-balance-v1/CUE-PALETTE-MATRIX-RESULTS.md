# Remembered-pain readout: fresh counterbalanced feasibility block

The frozen current-main CPU research binary completed all 128 predeclared cases: two fresh seeds 2026111554–1555 × safe-color mapping × red test side × exposure order × matched/different-color history × authored remembered-pain readout off/on × normal/fast-write-sham. All 128 had four actual consumed training exposures, observed positive pain on harmful food and zero pain on safe food, a unique test-food consumption, exact cold-next-step continuation and unchanged founder optimizer. The probe foods were both harmless. The cases share two seeds and repeated conditions; they are not 128 independent genotypes or a statistical population sample. An interrupted batch was resumed by validating and retaining completed case files and retrying only an incomplete fresh case; no user life or trained checkpoint was touched.

For each fast-write mode in the matched red/blue history, the original readout chose the formerly safe cue in 4/16 setups, while the authored remembered-pain readout did so in 12/16. Paired conditions show eight unsafe→safe shifts, four safe→safe and four unsafe→unsafe; none shifted safe→unsafe. The entire improvement came from seed 1554 (0/8→8/8); seed 1555 stayed 4/8 in both arms. Normal and sham made the same choices for every corresponding case. This points to the existing episodic memory/readout path, not evidence that waking fast synaptic writes produced a learned preference. The 48 readout weights were authored from zero, not learned.

In all 64 different-color-history control cases, both variants consumed blue; those red/blue probe colors had no previously safe assignment in that condition, and the hypothetical safe-color label did not affect the physical choice. This exposes a strong baseline blue choice in these conditions. Different-color histories share food category, taste, material and outcome experiences with test foods and can generalize, so they are not an isolated no-memory control. In the matched histories, the candidate's switches are consistent with cue-dependent episodic outcome recall in seed 1554, but alternative context or ordering effects remain possible and the other seed did not show a physical improvement. Do not describe this as reliable across creatures or as proof of general learning.

`cue-palette-matrix-summary.json` gives one compact row per case with mapping, position, order, history, readout and sham flags; physical choice; all four exposure times and pain deltas; fast-state count; restore/optimizer checks; first-probe red-minus-blue Ingest score; and SHA-256 of the complete local receipt. All 64 normal trials had changed fast state and all 64 sham trials had zero fast writes. Further independent seeds, reversed contingencies, longer delay, unrelated-pain/food safety and current GPU gameplay qualification are needed before any promotion. No default founder, user life, trained checkpoint or GPU runtime was changed; all environment switches are research-only.
