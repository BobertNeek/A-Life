# Different-color experience control: feasibility pilot

One fresh CPU seed 2026111553, red-safe/red-left and safe-first, normal fast writes, with Approach70. Four cases compared matched red/blue vs different-color green/gray-green exposure histories and Ingest remembered-pain gain 0 vs aggregate -4. Both training palettes maintained the same safe/harmful schedule, identical primary positive chemical cues and actual physical outcomes; green/gray-green are symmetric in red and blue components, but share food category and other cues and may generalize. Each case consumed all four exposures, safe pain 0 and harmful pain approximately 0.240/0.232; all had exact cold-next and unchanged-founder-optimizer checks.

With matched red/blue training, the baseline consumed formerly harmful blue on probe decision 2; the -4 remembered-pain readout consumed formerly safe red on decision 4. With different-color training, both versions consumed red on decision 2; red and blue were not previously experienced in that condition and neither is labeled as previously safe. This is one new seed and one configuration, not proof of an association or general benefit. The test shows that both histories can produce real exposures and choices, and that a genotype-level readout edit can affect a physical choice in this one matched case. Different training colors can change other state or cues, and all fast/memory effects are confounded within this small pilot.
