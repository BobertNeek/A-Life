Native CPU research only. Source is base 205f60c200dc05f4f0ae8c02d3f5b9747dbc0abd plus `narrow-reference.patch`. The dev executable built successfully. No claim is made that the separate cargo-test suite passed; its earlier attempt was stopped for storage pressure. The patch includes per-position before/after recording and rejects non-action-decoder changes in the edited arms. The broad calibration edits 10 weights relative to whole-care, the narrow arm edits seven, preserving the three Approach-reference positions.

The prospective comparison finished all 24 trials: two fresh seeds (2026111504 and 2026111505), four scenes, three arms, 32 steps. All three arms consumed nearby food 2/2. For distant food, whole-care consumed 1/2 and both edited arms consumed 0/2. In seed 1504 the narrow creature moved toward the food, ending at x=1.604, while the target was at x=2.5 and remained active. It had not reached/passed the food; the user was promptly sent this correction after an earlier mistaken statement. This is a short cutoff, not proof of permanent failure. Neither edited arm improves this basic task in this small sample.

For harmless bitter-tasting food, whole-care consumed 0/2 and each edited arm 1/2; whether refusal is desirable is context-dependent and this is not a learned aversion test. Toys remaining active do not imply failure. All 24 reported exact cold-next-step continuation and unchanged founder optimizer. Trajectories diverge once sampled actions differ, so these tiny matched samples are diagnostic, not a statistical performance conclusion.

For seed 1504 distant food, the original selected an Ingest-family representative on steps 15, 28 and 29, while the narrow arm selected Approach on all 32 steps. Ingest-family choice alone is not proof of physical eating; the outcome record and target state establish the original's consumption. The narrow correction still changes two Ingest reference weights (indices 26927: 30→6, 27135: -60→-12), making that a targeted next investigation, not a proven causal diagnosis. Keep the original rather than promote either edit, and test the approach-to-ingestion handoff with one family changed at a time. No learned life, accepted default, or user checkpoint was overwritten, and no GPU or biological learning claim is made.
