# Neutral-food screening and fast-write control

Current-main c3aec16 plus `neutral-food-sham.patch` and the two pinned research example modules. Sixteen fresh CPU trials: four paired seeds 2026111542–1545 × original/boosted experimental founders × normal/sham fast writes, 64 neural decisions. The single reachable object is harmless nutritious food (nutrition 0.6) with primary raw chemical cue exactly 0, not +0.85. There is no forced food action or extra brain circuit. Initial body energy was 0.4 and newborn hunger was actually 0.0, so non-consumption is not evidence of starvation despite low body energy.

For each founder and each mode, one of four seeds (1543) consumed the neutral food within 64 decisions; the other three left it active. This establishes that real neutral-food exposure is possible but inconsistent in this small newborn fixture. It does not establish a defect in a hungry creature or a learned preference. The boost changed no outcomes here; its edited coordinates influence the primary taste signal, which is neutral in this scenario.

Across eight matched normal/sham pairs, all sampled representative/channel choices matched in seven. In seed 1545 with the boosted founder, the pair first differed on decision 30: normal selected Move/Avoid and sham selected Move/Approach with the same offered action identities; both physical outcomes were movement and neither ate within the bound. Thus the fast-write intervention produced a real trajectory-level action difference in this bounded matched experiment, not merely a numerical logit difference. Because other memory, predictor, physiology and time evolve and trajectories may diverge after intervention, this does not prove useful learning or a learned cue-outcome association. The sham computes candidate waking updates but suppresses their fast writes; no equal wall-time claim is made. Every trial reported exact cold-next-step and unchanged founder optimizer. Normal fast coordinates changed in 28,200–28,920 entries by trial; sham fast entries remained unchanged.

`neutral-food-summary.json` records pair identities, initial hunger, choices and hashes of retained full local receipts. No user life, default foundation or trained checkpoint was altered. The next genuine learning test requires repeated actual exposures with counterbalanced neutral visual cues, adverse/reward outcomes, a later unforced choice and normal/sham controls; these pilot data are not that test.
