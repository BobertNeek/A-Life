This is native CPU research, not the default brain, a retained creature, GPU gameplay, training or learning. The candidate changes only existing action-decoder reference weights; it adds no wiring. The source is base 205f60c200dc05f4f0ae8c02d3f5b9747dbc0abd plus the adjacent `experiment.patch`; its executable hash and pilot are recorded separately. The dev executable built successfully. A later attempted cargo test build was interrupted because temporary storage was filling; no cargo-test pass is claimed.

Two matched fresh seeds, 2026111502 and 2026111503, four scene types, two weight sets, 32 steps per trial: 16 trials completed. Both controllers consumed nearby positive food in both seeds. For distant positive food, the original consumed it in one of two, the balanced candidate in zero of two. Neither consumed bitter food in the short bound. The toy is not food and its remaining active is not a failure. Actual trajectories may diverge after the first different sample, so these are small paired observations, not a statistically established general performance change or proof of avoidance. All 16 reported exact cold-next-step continuation and unchanged founder optimizer. The two tested seeds had initial energy 0.4 and no biology preparation ticks.

An earlier one-seed 12-step pilot had nearby food consumed by both, and distant food consumed by neither. The matched trials use disposable fresh founders, not a checkpoint migrated from a user's creature. Baseline whole-care altered 29,264 of 32,768 positions relative to that seed's source genetic weights; that number is not the number of calibrated positions, and is not acquired learning. The changed operating point is not promoted, because this small comparison supplies no demonstrated gain and shows a plausible distant-food regression. Keep the original and investigate a narrower correction rather than stacking more systems.

`comparison-summary.json` records outcomes, candidate identities and SHA-256 of the original individual receipts; full raw local receipts and temporary fresh candidate files remain local. No trained checkpoints, credentials or private creature memories are included in this source-only handoff. Paper motivation: https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/.

Post-run trace inspection: in seed 2026111503's far-food case, the two sampled trajectories first differed on the very first decision. The original selected Approach/Move and the calibrated arm selected Rest. On that frame, the original's Approach logit was 9.37765 while the calibrated arm's was 0.00232; Avoid changed from -7.58460 to +1.30354. The two candidate lists matched, but different sampled joint decisions and the resulting later world histories prevent attributing the endpoint to a single weight or action. These effects suggest separating Approach from the other family-reference changes in the next test rather than declaring every reference correction bad.
