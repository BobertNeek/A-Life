# Forced recovery sleep and individual state pilot

Current main c3aec16 plus the published `sleep-retention.patch` and the two pinned example-only support modules. Binary SHA-256 7a1b05e2dc66907976fde09dc8f6d528ef01e785fb3ec289e7273e7b832739ab. One new seed 2026111540, nearby food, original and two-weight boost, 16 ordinary neural decisions before an explicitly requested recovery sleep on a disposable cloned host. This is not autonomous sleep, a production change, a user creature, or GPU evidence.

Both arms went through ForcedRecoverySleep, Consolidating, Waking and Awake and resumed a real decision after 14 intervals. In original/boost respectively, 26,448/26,464 fast entries and the same number of lifetime entries changed between the pre-sleep state and consolidation; maximum lifetime deltas were 0.00172870/0.00163514. Pre-sleep lifetime was zero in these fresh trials; weight generation went 16→17 at consolidation and 18 after the resumed waking decision. Eligibility changed in the same counts. The post-wake serialized restore and next-step state matched exactly for both. The original pre-sleep host and user lives were not altered.

This demonstrates sleep consolidation and continuation of a fresh individual numerical state in these two bounded CPU trials. It does not establish autonomous sleep choice, that every pre-sleep fast change survives, that a learned behavior is preserved, or beneficial learning. The forced request is a test trigger, and the detached replay remains separately labeled. No candidate was promoted. `sleep-retention-summary.json` contains scalar results and hashes of local full receipts, not private neural arrays or trained checkpoints.
