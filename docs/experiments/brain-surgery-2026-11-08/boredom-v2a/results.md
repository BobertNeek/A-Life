# Frozen v2 results

## Conclusion and limits

The bounded candidate now has a measured graded boredom → grounded Play choice
→ real stimulation/relief loop, and its acquired consequence reader reduces
repeated failed Play. This is narrow CPU fixture evidence, not promotion to a
default brain, GPU qualification, autonomous mastery, or proof across genetics.

There were 60 controlled policy episodes and 2,016 actual native CPU decisions:
48 × 24-tick matrix episodes, eight 96-tick toy extensions, and four 24-tick
hunger-choice controls. Streams 17/29 reuse one fixed inherited candidate.
Detached frozen replays are not counted as acted policy episodes or learning.
The predeclared plan and compiled source/executable hashes are preserved.
Unless explicitly called an outcome tick, tick labels below identify the world
tick before the action interval. Receipts retain both decision and outcome ticks.

## What changed

86 genes differ from the immediate source candidate: four previously unused
recurrent edges, two contact-conditioned action readouts, and 80 normalized
copies of two native memory inputs. Current boredom is Homeostasis 9, neuron 265;
the independent path is 265→1961→898→1842 with signed tonic input 1967→898.

four edges, 4/2 on the action/reference readouts, and total 12/-16 on acquired
Other valence/danger. No world type, toy flag or high-level goal enters this
policy. The asset is fresh and profile 2-bound; no new sensory ABI is needed.

The source baseline is the earlier recalibrated drive-balance candidate, with
birth dendrite gain 0.5. It is not accepted v12's gain 6 brain. Every arm shares
the qualified Rest-translation correction. No accepted defaults or retained

## Matched failed Play and acquired evidence

The corrected non-playable target has identical initial sensory channels, body,
chemistry and candidate features, including contact 1, and identical initial
scores. Its world capability differs. It has no actual hazard dose; no chemistry
or hazard rule was edited.

Full attempted failed Play once on each stream; the reader ablation repeated
it 7/4 times. Neither received boredom relief. Stream 17's failed receipt was
followed by confidence-weighted valence -0.027743 and danger 0.028110; Play score
fell +0.298477→-0.429383. Stream 29 fell +0.359102→-0.468716. This is acquired
exact-action episodic evidence, not an optimizer update or a generic Other
reward. Inherited memory readers, memory storage, novelty and native plasticity
remain active in the reader-ablation arm.

The named no-drive CLI arm removes the entire added motivation module: four
bridge edges AND two contact readouts. It is not a single-input-edge lesion.
Therefore its behavior contrast does not isolate the input edge alone. The
graded frozen pressure intervention does isolate current boredom input at fixed
context; a single-edge acted lesion remains additional work if needed.

Native novelty also matters: successful Play assigns investigation=novelty and
scales stimulation by 1−novelty. A Play can execute and touch without boredom
relief. The tired stream 17
fixture had such a contact, consistent with this source mechanism. The probe
did not record the per-object novelty or investigation-dose scalar. Only the
physical receipt and resulting boredom/curiosity changes are measured; no
reconstructed novelty is labeled as measured. Physical execution, preparation,
failure and biological relief must not be conflated.

## Longer trajectories, rest and feeding

Over 96 ticks the full arm executed 9/10 Play contacts and ended boredom at
0.0066/0.0058; source baseline executed 4/6. No continuous-play latch appeared.
However, contact was absent late, so cessation alone does not prove relief was
the only cause. At contact 1 in full stream 17, observed scores fell with pressure:
+0.4807 at boredom 0.6683 (tick 8), +0.1381 at 0.3406 (tick 30), and -0.1115 at 0.0002
(tick 39), while acquired valence remained positive. Occasional low-drive Play
remained possible. These trajectory observations are not a fixed-state causal
substitute for the separately labeled pressure probes.

High-fatigue full fixtures selected Rest in 23/24 intervals on stream 17 and
24/24 on stream 29, matching source baseline. Stationary Rest produced measured
Body.sleeping in 14/24 and 13/24 intervals. Moving Rest correctly lost recovery.
Actual fatigue only fell 0.8→0.799040 in all four arms: zero developmental
expression gates the fatigue emitters, leaving slow passive decay. See
fatigue-diagnosis.md and the per-interval scalar receipts. This does not fix
recovery or test the separately calibrated wake/recovery chemistry.

All four full/source hunger-choice fixtures first made Touch at decision tick 0,
then actually Consumed during decision tick 1 (receipt outcome tick 2). They ended
at hunger 0.300330. No feeding delay was observed between those matched arms;
four fixtures cannot establish general hunger competence, distance navigation,
or whole-care policy quality. This matrix begins in contact with food.

## Preservation and qualification

- Six source/identity/grounding/chemistry/handling guards passed. Movable-toy
  pickup is preparation with zero stimulation; a later actual Play stimulates.
- Four common rest_motion_ guards passed.
- All 60 episodes preserved nonzero source optimizer moments, integer ages and
  step bit-for-bit, and round-tripped sealed acquired checkpoints byte-for-byte.
- Native fast weights were nonzero in every episode. This shows that ordinary
  local plasticity ran, not that synaptic learning caused the observed benefit.
  Lifetime/consolidation and all retained state were left to native semantics.
- No retained private checkpoint was read. No GPU device was used. No production
  default, accepted brain, or existing acquired-state bank was replaced.
- The initial saturated proposal and over-suppressed revision remain recorded,
  including the rejected obstacle control whose contact feature did not match.

Full receipts and row-level analysis remain in the experiment archive. The
source patch contains the reproducible probe, guards, analysis and this report;
it contains no learned checkpoints or executable binaries.

## Review artifacts and publication boundary

The local evidence archive retains every original output, including memory banks,
and the executable. It is for local review only. A separate publication-safe
bundle includes only allowlisted source and finite scalar/physical receipts,
with source-derived fields explicitly labeled. It omits checkpoints, memory
