# Addendum to the frozen biological-drive study

This separately versioned projection reads existing disposable fixture records only. The original summary, manifest, archive, source and chronological notes remain unchanged. No genes, lives, builds or native reruns were added.

- GroundedObjectSlotsV1 exposes distant chemistry in these fixtures. Terrain-only contact calibration must not be attributed to this sensor profile.
- Previously stated taste/distance coefficients were nominal, before the inherited v12 blanket ActionCandidate scaling by0.25. Actual Ingest taste/distance coefficients are3/−1.5 for baseline, gain-only and reference-recalibrated;0.6/−4 for soft geometry;3/−20 for distance-only.
- The bitter-plus-seeded-pain scene uses harmless nutritious food with hazard_pain=0. Its result is a response to seeded internal pain and bitter chemistry, not injury avoidance, learned harm recall or a causal harm-learning assay.
- “Pending” passages in earlier notes describe the chronology of experiments, not the present qualification status.
- actions-v2.json exposes exact selected candidate action IDs, families, kinds and generated targets alongside recorded physical channel outcomes. It cannot independently establish the resolved Move/Interact primitive because that field was not recorded. Per-channel success and persistent per-tick consumed state also remain unknown. Aggregate success, exact Consumed contact events and final entity consumption are separately available. Null unknowns are never interpreted as false.

See schema-v2.json for field rules and source-bindings-v2.json for exact inputs and source hashes. project.py reproduces the projection without running the simulation. All identifiers and positions are generated disposable fixture data; biochemical private state is omitted.
