# Recovery/motion candidate, source-only qualification

## Mechanism and limits

This is a separately owned CPU research sampler candidate, not a deployed neural motor lobe or learned skill. Its unchanged 256-cell native research kernel provides the group logit and operation logits. The new administrative factor uses sigmoid(native group logit), an observed-geometry actuator displacement prediction, and a raw-homeostatic escape guard. It does not inspect actual future movement, world object kind, nutrition, hidden positions, or private memory banks. Closed learning and original source forward are retained.

Feature = sigmoid(group logit) × clamp(predicted translation / .1, 0, 1) × indicator(representative or posture is RestGroup) × (1 − max(clamp((fear−.25)/.75,0,1), clamp((pain−.1)/.9,0,1))). Weight is fixed −2. Geometry confidence attenuates translation prediction; absent/uncertain observed geometry adds no constraint. This is a modest inherited diagnostic prior, not a fitted or biologically validated coefficient. At baseline fear/pain it discourages incompatible motion. At maximal fear or pain the new energy is exactly zero. Neither movement nor recovery choices are masked or overwritten. Both recovery operations get identical compatibility, so their native conditional relative probability is unchanged; pressure remains the native operation selector.

The raw guard is deliberately identified as administrative, not as the actual native positive-opponent activations. The earlier observed group logit .171631 gives sigmoid ≈ .5428, so maximum added penalty is approximately −1.086 at baseline threat, not −2 unconditionally. A larger group score increases coordination strength but need does not force Rest. Unknown geometry and imperfect actuator predictions can still yield unsuccessful recovery; only physical receipts establish success.

For each representative, reuse the geometric P(L,M|r). Compute posture Q(P|L,r) and its partition once per unique L. Reweight each L/M pair by that posture partition, then reweight representative mass by the integrated pair partition. This is exact Gibbs normalization of the original joint law plus recovery energy; it does not enumerate the full L×M×P Cartesian product in production. Operation mass integrates to 1 and unmodified channel factors integrate to 1. Selected latent-path gradients are (feature − global mean)/temperature. A physical-outcome marginal must sum all matching latent paths; the diagnostic tests do this rather than confusing selected-path probability with physical-outcome probability.

Selected-event counters expose geometric features/energy products, posture features, pair/representative reweights, normalization terms and exponentials per distribution construction. They are not comprehensive FLOPs, elapsed time, or body ATP billing. sample/evaluate/validation repeatedly construct distributions. Total administration is still unpriced biologically and cannot support an equal-compute comparison or zero-cost claim. A future native implementation needs its own circuit, weight ownership, billing and qualification rather than silently relabeling this prototype.
