# Grounded need-specific consequence proposal (not implemented)

## What the V4 reader actually read

The appended head5 reads candidate decoder input32, the first family-value port, not the target-latent ports24..31. `memory/candidate_recall.rs::record_from_patch` constructs family_value[0] from `memory_consequence(outcome)`. With measured physiology this is experienced_valence, an aggregate of measured biological consequences; without it the legacy fallback is reward_valence. The other three family entries are success, danger and energy. The query records action ID/kind/family, tracked object, profile and contextual features. `recall_family_channel_with_budget` and `aggregate_family_matches` select and similarity-weight eligible records. The decoder receives this acquired family value with its confidence semantics, not a secret peer type.

The measured common locomotor contribution comes from this single aggregate family port under an equal .35 coefficient for the eight family rows, multiplied by one common loneliness source. It is not evidence that the exact same source record was recalled for all candidates; the safe receipt did not retain per-row best-source identities. Final replay candidate inputs verify the port values, and exact masks/logits verify the common-mode consequence. Do not invent historical record IDs or isolated social-effect provenance. The experienced valence can combine contact relief, passive drift, energy and other changes in a joint interval.

## Smallest honest new information contract

Introduce a separately named optional candidate consequence record/recall version, only for fresh explicitly selected recipes. Preserve existing V2 records, byte formats, old port coordinates, canonical digests and readers. The new record stores an observed signed before→after homeostatic delta vector with an explicit measured-physiology availability flag, alongside action family, target identity where actually observed, context/profile, timestamp, confidence and source sequence. Missing measurement is Unknown, not zero. Store raw signed deltas without declaring all11 to be deficits: ATP availability, curiosity/reproductive urges and reserved lanes retain distinct meanings.

For a first narrow implementation, loneliness alone plus a valid-bit is sufficient; a full11 vector is more general but carries greater storage/index/serialization cost. The full-vector alternative must bind its schema and units, never infer missing old components. Old V2 may continue answering old queries, while new need-specific queries return Unknown until genuinely observed new consequences exist. A sidecar can minimize old-record changes but adds synchronization, pruning and restore obligations; a versioned record is simpler to validate but requires explicit dual-store admission and larger serialization review. Neither option migrates old valence into fictional loneliness relief.

Recall should condition the new observation on action family AND an observed target/context association. Start with exact observed target+family matches and explicit confidence; a later perceptual similarity generalization is separately testable. No built-in peer/toy label, global social preference, or reuse of target-only pooled deltas as family-specific evidence. For joint actions this remains associative interval outcome, not proof that one channel caused the delta. Store joint-action provenance or mark association accordingly rather than duplicate full relief as independent causal credit for every channel.

A separate appended/versioned decoder port can consume this signed observed delta. For loneliness, lower after−before means relief, so the readout sign should favor negative observed delta when current pressure is high and disfavor worsening. Keep existing aggregate adverse-outcome/safety readouts rather than replacing them with a single need score. No uniform negative mapping for all eleven lanes.

## Finite test sequence before any acted allocation

1. Serialization/identity: pinned old bytes/digests and old restore unchanged; new schema/source/profile/recipe exact admission; malformed and missing measurements fail closed or produce Unknown as defined; no invented migration.
2. Disposable fixed world observations: same target before/after real contact versus equally physical non-social control; passive no-contact drift; matched family/target swaps and joint-action provenance. Measure the physiological vector from sealed interval observations, not a target label.
3. Recall: exact target+family associations, unrelated targets/families, empty/Unknown records, confidence/age, merge/pruning and no duplicated counts. Confirm old family-value ports remain unchanged.
4. Detached native scoring: fixed acquired positive relief, worsening and Unknown/empty memories at artificial pressure0/.05/.15 with actual encoder/effects, source/metabolic bounds and1/8/32 horizons. Check monotonic sign, effective-zero ablation, conditional pairwise logit effects, gradient/eligibility/credit, CPU/WGSL packing and explicit offline kernel parity where available.
5. Only after those results, freeze a separate finite ordinary-world comparison with no forced movement/contact and no adaptive extension. V4's512 intervals stay closed.

A small offline coefficient1/2 diagnostic could quantify sensitivity of the existing aggregate reader, but is not the next behavioral remedy and receives no additional acted arm. The new information contract is a larger compatibility change requiring focused review; this document proposes it rather than claiming completion or permission to reinterpret existing lives.
