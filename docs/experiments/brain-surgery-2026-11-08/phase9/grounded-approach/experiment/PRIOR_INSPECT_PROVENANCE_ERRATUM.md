# Prior Inspect fixture: precise provenance clarification

Applies to the inherited PR310 source and documentation, not a rerun or a change
to its measured physical outcomes. The prior fixture really executed registered
Inspect intervals and measured canonical before/after biology. Its passive clones
really matched the social relief, so it did not isolate Inspect causality.

However, `headless/need_consequence_evidence_tests.rs::measured_fixture` manually
constructs `NeuralActionSelection` with logit0, confidence.9 and zero active tiles
and synapses, then calls `DecisionSnapshot::from_cpu_neural_selection`. That is a
prescribed action-envelope **contract fixture**, not an actual CPU neural decision.
The matching `PreActionSnapshot::from_cpu_neural_frame` also gives a schema label,
not evidence that any forward/select pass took place. Structural validation of
that label does not authenticate its provenance.

Exact wording to read with this qualification:

- `NEED_EVIDENCE_RESULTS_V1.md`, “All11 stored deltas match the actual measured
  physiology bitwise. Measured zero remains known.” These are storage/lookup
  results for prescribed contract envelopes containing real biological samples.
  They do not show that a genuine neural producer supplied admitted evidence.
- Same file, “Actual next-world queries miss in all3 cases.” The query frames
  came from the real successor worlds; the stored source used the synthetic
  neural-labeled envelope just described. This is an exact-matcher compatibility
  limit in that contract fixture, not an autonomous creature's recall result.
- `README.md`, “Exact source-context recall and byte-exact restore pass.” This is
  a valid contract-level pass only. The nearby “real selection” future gate is
  still open and must not be omitted from later summaries.
- `README.md`, “Only a validated sealed observation with measured physiology and
  an observed representative target can be admitted.” Add: V1 also requires the
  neural-origin schema. Its producer must supply genuine neural provenance;
  validation alone cannot establish that the producer actually ran a network.
- `NEED_EVIDENCE_INTERFACE.md`, “Tests explicitly distinguish synthetic contract
  fixtures from actual registered-world measurements.” Both can coexist in one
  fixture: the body measurements are actual, while its neural decision envelope

The new Approach fixture does not repeat that envelope construction. It retains
real operation/physiology evidence as prescribed observations, leaves the
neural-only store empty, and labels exact-source and next-source recalls Unknown.
No admission rule is relaxed and no additional neural policy run is authorized.
