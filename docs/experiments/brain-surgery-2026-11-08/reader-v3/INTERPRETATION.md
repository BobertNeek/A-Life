# How to interpret v3

This is a passive study of one inherited OFF brain with two fresh sampling identities and counterbalanced H/S positions. It is not a five-arm behavioral experiment. Only OFF actions reach the world; all other gain choices are instantaneous same-state/same-RNG-draw counterfactual selections. They do not describe alternate future physiology or learning.

All naturally acquired recurrent state, memory context and effective offsets are shared for a given decision. The operation port reads the confidence-weighted Ingest-family projection for the handled target. Therefore family32/confidence on an operation is the projected recall context, not proof that the stored operation outcome changed family. Source-family, exact-consumption and tracked-target flags must be considered together before calling an episode an exact remembered ingestion of that target.

The decisive data questions are whether the port naturally becomes nonzero; whether evidence is bound to actual ingestion; the scale of real action/innate/target-memory scores; how learned coefficients differ from inherited ones; whether clipping suppresses the added readout; and whether low-gain probability or instantaneous choice changes occur. Actual meal, heldness and physical receipts describe OFF only.

Acquired offsets can invalidate an inherited bound on the *absolute* operation contribution. However, these offsets are identical between shadows, so they do not invalidate the instantaneous reader-minus-OFF perturbation bound: at gain1/16, one candidate's logit shift is≤.5 for valid |input53|≤1, subject to floating-point tolerance. Both candidates changing can alter their pairwise margin by≤1. Neither fact guarantees stable stochastic decisions or better outcomes.

An engaged port or changed sample is not a benefit. A poisonous-to-eat object may be useful to move. No useful-handling task oracle is supplied to the brain here, and no counterfactual physical benefit can be inferred merely from fewer pickups. No winner or promotion is justified solely by this data.

Partial accounting distinguishes requested steps, returned host steps, and fully projected/validated rows. A returned step whose projection fails still consumed exposure and is not retried. If no harmful ingestion occurs naturally, absence of an aversive-operation signal is an engagement limitation, not a reason to extend or force exposure.
