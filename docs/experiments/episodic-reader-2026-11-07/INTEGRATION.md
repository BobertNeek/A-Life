## Why this is the next surgery

The whole-brain candidate could reach food and distinguish the supplied taste
conditions, but repeated harmful-food experience did not reliably change its
choices. We traced two bottlenecks instead of declaring that it had learned:

1. The inherited genes did not read acquired episodic value strongly enough.
   The accepted memory correction was capped at 0.5 logits.
2. Benign preparatory Ingest outcomes could temporarily replace the remembered
   consequence of an actual meal. PR #299 fixes that retrieval rule while
   preserving Unknown legacy records, old digests, and save continuation.

This research candidate has a distinct foundation/selector identity and a
versioned plan capped at 12. The cap is shared: episodic and cognitive decoder
contributions are each clipped separately by it. Thus this ABI also relaxes
cognitive-head clipping where that head is present. The existing 0.5 plan is
unchanged. Valid N2048 gain candidates have no cognitive channel or
CognitiveContext decoder coordinates: the cognitive extension is admitted only
for the separate N512 action-credit candidate. Source review therefore rules
out a cognitive-scoring confound in this frozen N2048 factor. The integration
adds an explicit regression assertion for that absence; it is not yet rerun.
Genetic
target fear/pain readouts were fixed in v11. V12 adds precisely 40 Ingest
family-value readout weights, each 0 to 0.8, for an aggregate +32 coefficient.
Those inherited weights read memory that ordinary experience updates. They are
not weights learned by Adam, and no actor optimizer update is claimed.

## Completed development pilot

The four arms share one episode seed, continuous native CPU physiology,
ordinary plasticity and sampling. They differ only in the qualified outcome
phase correction and the family-value reader. All results are development
evidence, not held-out generalization.

- Legacy retrieval, reader absent: 3 harmful A meals, 4 safe B meals, 82 ticks.
- Legacy retrieval, reader present: 3 A, 5 B, 128 ticks.
- Corrected retrieval, reader absent: 3 A, 4 B, 82 ticks.
- Corrected retrieval, reader present: 2 A, 8 B, the predeclared 256-tick limit.

The combined arm had 182 post-harm A-contact opportunities and 107 paired
contact rows. On actual rows 128 and 192, removing only the acquired family
value input changes the immutable native sampled choice to Ingest(A). Bodily
pain is zero at row 192. This demonstrates a local causal contribution from
acquired episodic recall. It does not demonstrate an alternate future rollout,
learned synaptic avoidance, generalization, reversal, or reliable self-care.

The remaining failure is material: at row 149 the creature deliberately grabs
A, then keeps it while making 69 unsuccessful attempts to ingest B. It still
moves and avoids further A bites, but its final safe meal was at row 142. Do not
score this as solved feeding or survival.

See [the frozen v12 report](notes/v12-native-learning.md), including negative
results and the invalid stale-artifact preflight that was caught before lives
ran. The fresh read-only review independently checked source/binary/asset
bindings, the 40 gene differences, all 496 primary and 53 family-only sampled
choices, opportunity counts, and the late grip trap. It did not reopen private
checkpoint payloads. Private state integrity is supported by the assay's
completed checks and source guards.

## Qualification limits and next steps

The frozen candidate passed CPU admission/cross-restore tests with nonzero
acquired neural state. The GPU checks cover plan upload, bucket admission and
WGSL validation only. No GPU execution or CPU/GPU numerical parity at gain 12
has been measured. Existing accepted/default assets and acquired lives were
not opened or migrated. Ordinary live-game N2048 admission still rejects this
identity; the implementation exposes explicit CPU and direct GPU research
admission only. It is not gameplay/default promotion.

The proposed next test is eight new, paired fresh lives with frozen gene bytes,
four new episode seeds and counterbalanced initial sides. The dose, safety
limits, sleep rule, sampler and coefficients must remain fixed. No-exposure
lives remain untested, and held-object deadlocks remain self-care failures.
Local disk headroom cannot safely fit another assay link while retaining the
agreed free-space floor, so a bounded hosted run is being assessed. No further
cache deletion is part of this plan.

Before designing release circuitry, trace the exact profile's observability.
Grab and release currently share a Contact candidate, and the ObjectSlots
profile has no explicit candidate-bound heldness bit. Do not add a hidden
hazard-aware automatic drop rule to make this pilot pass. A grounded
proprioceptive or affordance extension must have its own identity, CPU/GPU
semantics, controls and compatibility tests.
