# Episodic reader research and Astra handoff

This is an opt-in fresh-founder CPU research candidate. Accepted/default brains,
existing acquired lives, checkpoints and optimizer history are not replaced or
migrated. It is not admitted to ordinary live gameplay.

Start with [the integration report](INTEGRATION.md) for the design, measured
results, corrections to historical prose, compatibility and GPU limits.

## Completed evidence

- [V10: ordinary lifetime credit](notes/v10-native-learning.md) did not produce
  sustained avoidance.
- [V11: stronger target-memory readout](notes/v11-native-learning.md) affected
  choices but still did not solve repeated harmful feeding.
- [V12: acquired family value and outcome phase](notes/v12-native-learning.md)
  produced 2 harmful / 8 safe meals over 256 ticks in one development episode.
  Same-state family-input ablation establishes a local causal contribution from
  acquired recall. A later held-object trap prevents a reliable self-care claim.
- [The fixed-brain geometry factor](geometry-factor/RESULTS.md) separates a world
  correction from unchanged v5/v6 genes. Those gains are not learning evidence.

## Partial held-out replication and next design

[The hosted study](hosted-results/RESULTS.md) completed qualification, then
validated seven of eight attempted lives before failing closed at the final
reporting gate. Three complete pairs support less repeated harmful feeding
with the fixed reader, but all three reader-on lives ended holding harmful A
and failed safe-food attempts. The fourth reader-on outcome is unknown. The
study is closed without rerun; this is not reliable self-care or promotion.
[The protocol](HOSTED-TRANSFER.md) and exact sanitized partial are preserved.

[Grounded grip context](GRIP-NEXT-DESIGN.md) is a separate, unimplemented next
hypothesis. It does not add an automatic poison-aware drop reflex.

The source includes a distinct gain-plan identity, CPU restoration guards,
GPU upload/WGSL validation support, shared candidate construction, and the
bounded assay. GPU device execution and numerical parity remain unmeasured.
No private checkpoints, diagnostics, journals, neural banks or optimizer banks
are bundled. Frozen asset hashes identify genetic inputs without distributing
those files.
