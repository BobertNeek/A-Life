# Fixed brains × corrected body geometry

## Result

The world correction changes the brain comparison materially. With the same archived genetic weights, v5 completes the obstacle meal in 1/4 old-world trials and 4/4 corrected-world trials. V6 completes 4/4 in either world and remains faster in every corrected-world pair. Open reaching is exactly unchanged, not merely similar.

The earlier two-weight result remains valid in its original world. It is not evidence that brain weights alone caused every navigation failure. Correcting a physically inconsistent body envelope can rescue an unchanged controller.

## Frozen design and source

Each trial runs 120 native CPU host steps, initial energy 0.4, no biological preparation, harmless positive food at 2.5 m and ordinary somatic learning. ## Interpretation and limits

The geometry correction uses authored obstacle radius plus declared body radius for actual motion, with explicit overlap recovery and existing conservative clearance steering. Hand/grip/contact and vision rules are not changed by this factor. The separate world tests establish actual clear/intersecting body-envelope behavior and old-save recovery; these 32 brain trials do not replace those checks.

The inherited visible-goal actuator already supplies bounded lateral clearance steering. These results do not establish newly learned detours or planning. They concern one hand-built weight set per arm, four sampling/body/world seeds, two visible-food scenes, a 120-step horizon, and the ObjectSlots profile. That profile exposes raw object chemistry at distance. There is no poison-learning, Terrain-language, broad navigation, GPU, acquired-skill migration or promotion claim.

The analysis initially assumed entity3 for both scenes. Independent preflight review caught that open far-food uses entity2; target resolution and serialized-f32 energy validation were corrected before interpreting any trials. This changed analysis only, not frozen source or candidates. The final independent audit recalculated the summary and all integrity checks and found no blocker.
