# Acquired family value × outcome-phase correction

## Result

The combined candidate demonstrates a causal, experience-dependent reduction in harmful-food ingestion in this native CPU development pilot. It is not a complete self-care or survival solution. The creature still takes a second harmful bite through stochastic sampling, and later becomes stuck holding that food while repeatedly trying to eat the safe alternative.

## What changed

The inherited target fear/pain reader and memory cap 12 were fixed from v11. The only new genetic coefficient was aggregate +32 on MemoryContext Ingest lane 32, equally divided over its 40 compiled coordinates. Every other genetic coordinate stayed bit-identical. This lane represents acquired, confidence-weighted expected value of that target/action family. It does not expose world hazard, target A/B labels or an innate poison oracle.

The world-source factor was exactly qualified PR299: known Consumed outcomes do not get replaced by benign preparation when retrieving tracked Ingest expectation. The two candidate assets were byte-identical across source revisions. No geometry patch was included. Native somatic plasticity, biochemistry, perception, recurrent state, sampling and ecology remained enabled and continuous; founder/Adam state did not update.

Each fresh life had the same 600 biological preparation ticks, energy 0.4, seed 2026110901, fixed equal positive food chemistry and nutrition 0.05. A hazard was 0.35 and B harmless, with distinct ordinary appearance and persistent tracked identity. Ordinary same-object regrowth occurred after 16 ticks. Limits were 256 host ticks, three harmful consumptions, health minimum 0.70 and injury maximum 0.25. Recovery sleep was requested immediately after first A consumption, independent of its measured credit.

A meals actually produced biological credit from -0.08645 to -0.08867 and measured aversive harm 0.28 across the factor. Safe B credit was positive. World reward_valence was zero. No decision or reward was fabricated.

## Causal evidence beyond being hurt or inactive

The combined arm made 224 decisions after first harm, with 207 visible A Ingest opportunities, 182 A contact opportunities and 107 simultaneous A/B contact rows. It selected Avoid(A) on 57 rows and Ingest(A) only once more, while selecting Ingest(B) on 83 rows and actually eating B seven times after first harm. These opportunity counts include ordinary moving and held-food encounters; they are not forced paired trials.

At index 50 both foods were in contact. Ingest(A) scored -8.077288 and Ingest(B) +4.058438. The actual joint action avoided A and ate B. Removing all explicit episodic decoder inputs on that immutable real row changes the sampled action to approach and ingest A.

More narrowly, zeroing only the Ingest-family value input on the exact real rows 128 and 192 changes the sampled non-ingesting action into Ingest(A). Recurrent state, physiological inputs, offsets, other memory inputs, gates, candidates, sampling seed/counter and every other field stay fixed. At index 192 current bodily pain is already zero. The A family-value input is -0.252217; its lane contributes -8.072283 logits. Native A Ingest score is -4.336601; removing that lane raises it to +3.735682 and changes the selected posture/inspection action into harmful ingestion. Thus this is a causal contribution from acquired family expectation, not a claim based only on nonzero banks, less movement or endpoint inactivity.

Independent arithmetic over captured fresh offsets and declared genes agrees with native detached logits within 0.0002. These immutable replays never mutate or continue the actual life. They establish the current causal effect of recalled experience; they are not a clean whole-life learning-off control.

## Why both factors matter

In reader-absent matched controls at index 56, target fear/pain are the same approximately -0.000525/0.0. Legacy family valence is +0.0000628 after benign preparation; corrected retrieval keeps it -0.241016. With no inherited family reader, Ingest score still stays about +4.03 and the creature eats A. The correct information alone has too little influence through the very small acquired readouts.

With +32 but legacy retrieval, the second meal is also at 56 and the third at 127; the latter has almost-zero family expectation and an A Ingest representative probability of 77.77%. Strong readout cannot help when the relevant outcome has been replaced by preparation in the retrieved summary.

With both factors, negative family value continues to suppress Ingest when the separate target fear/pain aggregate misses the old injury episode. At index 96 target pain is zero but family valence is -0.235077, adding -7.522470 logits. A Ingest scores -3.529011. The second harmful meal nevertheless occurs because its representative probability remains 0.204746% and that draw is sampled. The sampled Ingest representative forces manipulation while locomotion independently chooses Approach. This was neither a missing family signal nor a binding cap: raw memory effect was -7.589797, inside +/-12. No hard veto or sampling change was added.

## Possession is the remaining practical failure

The repertoire already offers Contact/GRAB action 211 for the held object. Executing it on the same held target releases it; there is no separate Drop candidate. The 24 object features expose generic contact but no target-specific possession/release flag. Grip is sensed globally and through pose/relative movement. A negative Contact fear/pain reader suppresses both acquisition and release unless other context distinguishes them.

The combined life did release A once: Grab at 128, release at 129. It then ate B three more times, last at 142. At 149 it deliberately selected Grab(A) again. Unlike an activity-held item, that explicit grip is protected from automatic Eat(B) placement by the existing hand rules. It remained held through 255.

Over indices 149–255 the creature took 107 decisions, with zero bodily pain throughout and 2.874465 m cumulative displacement. It selected Ingest(B) 69 times; all 69 manipulation outcomes had no contact/consumption and ordinary failure because the deliberate A grip could not be replaced. It never selected Ingest(A) in this interval. A was held at entry on 106 of the 107 rows, and no release was selected after the new acquisition. Both foods remained active at the endpoint, A held and B unheld.

Therefore the late plateau is not inactivity, missing A opportunities or simple satiation. It is learned ingestion suppression coupled to an unresolved possession/action-context problem. Eight safe meals and a healthier endpoint do not establish reliable continued self-care. The experiment does not demonstrate broken physical rules; no release mechanics were changed.

## Learning, sleep and preservation limits

All four lives requested sleep at world tick 619. Native consolidation occurred at index 27 and ordinary decisions resumed at 32. Combined-arm behavior continued for 224 post-sleep decisions. All 16 baseline/primary lives preserved exact founder/Adam bytes, exact final checkpoint cold roundtrip and exact next-step continuation. This supports the measured short continuity across actual sleep and restore, not unrelated acquired-skill retention or a reversal claim.

Final fast maxima were 0.003722, 0.004256, 0.003722 and 0.005092; lifetime maximum was about 0.003123 in all conditions. These remain far below bounds 2 and 4. The combined arm's frozen first-row A-minus-B margin changes by +0.000187874, not an acquired synaptic penalty against A. The useful discriminative effect is recall of experience through the inherited family reader, not new Adam training or a learned poison-color synapse.

This is one fixed development seed and one environment. No held-out seed, side/cue swap, reversal, unrelated-memory retention, whole-life no-update comparator, GPU hardware run, safe-food throughput guarantee or accepted/default brain promotion was tested. A rational next study should first replicate unchanged coefficients on held-out identities/sides, and treat possession-aware action context as a distinct candidate rather than extending the dose or merely raising the memory cap.
