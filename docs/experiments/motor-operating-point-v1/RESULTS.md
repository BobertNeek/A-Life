# Result: the readout correction is effective; attempted tonic compensation is null

This is a detached, fixed-context native CPU experiment, not a world rollout, trained policy or promoted brain. All eight weight candidates were compiled into their own exact experimental-graph phenotype and immutable asset identity. Existing assets, branches and acquired state were untouched.

## Qualification

- Five tests passed, including coordinate-by-coordinate equality, dynamics/encoder/decoder/projection/dendrite equality for all eight candidates, sign controls, old-bound asset rejection and new-bound admission.
- 160 factorial replay evaluations, 10,240 frame slots, 18 conditions per arm and three simultaneous independent decoder contexts. Three supplemental 64-frame replays measured the proposed tonic source directly.
- 144 exact input-cut sequence checks, 72 complete activation-trace comparisons between matched readout factors, 270 exact comparisons with the published frozen whole-care6 baseline, and 1,024 final-microstep motor-input reconstruction checks passed.
- No world or learning steps. The same fixed encoded inputs, projection gain 1 and threshold shift 0 are used throughout. These results do not describe every biochemical modulation state.

## Isolated readout effect

Changing Approach/Inspect from their frozen +5 reference contribution to a -1 differential reference leaves every recurrent activation exactly unchanged. In the balanced nutritious-contact fixture, Approach falls from **10.798813 to approximately 0.000130**, and Inspect from **2.699792 to approximately 0.000028**. This removes those two tonic readout preferences; it does not repair their saturated motor sensitivity or establish a good overall policy. Other frozen readout priors, including Avoid, remain.

Hunger's Ingest response remains +3.123727 and fatigue's Rest response remains only +0.000065446. The same missing neural axes from the baseline remain missing. There is no new low-ATP arbitration or social/play competence.

## Why the tonic sweep did nothing

Tonic-only gains 4, 6 and 8 produced **exactly identical sampled motor activations and family logits** to frozen whole-care6 over all 54 condition/context rows and six sampled latencies. The nominal positive-input / negative-Modulatory / positive-MotorProposal path was structurally valid, but it was not energized.

Direct native traces show source neuron **383** and reservoir **1967** at exactly zero through all 64 frames in this context. Both have zero bias and zero encoded input. Relay neurons 898 and 911 reach only **+7.935693e-7**, from residual network activity, not a negative tonic current. Multiplying a supposed tonic connection by a larger coefficient cannot create the assumed signal. This is not a failure of tanh to represent negative activity and does not prove that all counterbalancing or fresh branch stabilization must fail.

The null is scoped to this assay. Production biochemical input offsets/gains could energize an unassigned neuron differently; the label “tonic” does not establish its sign, magnitude or constancy there either. No ATP or sensory signal was silently relabeled as a constant.

## Measured operating-point contributions

A native replay of the identical prefix ending just before the fourth microstep was used to reconstruct that step, including exact old activation, metabolic load, inherited branch input and route cadence. At baseline Rest signal motor 1747:

- inherited dendritic sum: approximately **+5.97032**;
- ordinary recurrent sum: **+0.30183**;
- neuron bias: **+0.05**;
- homeostatic subtraction: approximately **−0.99990**;
- resulting activation: approximately **0.999949**.

Rest reference 1759 receives essentially zero ordinary recurrent sum but a similar +5.97032 branch contribution and remains approximately 0.999913. Thus the inherited branch contribution is directly measured as the dominant positive input at this operating point. This is stronger evidence than a positive weight or topology label, but it is not a branch-removal experiment or proof of stability in other contexts.

All baseline motor activations were identical at frames 32 and 64 in this fixture; the largest all-neuron drift was 4.47e-6. All-neuron drift from frames 48 to 64 was zero. These are measured convergence checks for this context only. Positive/negative activation ranges and saturation counts are retained in the summary.
