# Biological-drive routing: qualified detached CPU baseline

This archive preserves the corrected three-arm native replay experiment and its failures of neural drive coverage. The accepted graph and existing acquired creatures are unchanged. The experiment measured neural dynamics, not autonomous behavior, learning or GPU execution.

Read [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) for the findings and [README.md](README.md) for controls. The receipt binds the exact corrected source, executable and compressed results. The two fixture tests passed; the assay evaluated 144 sequences of 64 frames each. Its 135 input-cut sequence checks establish exact deterministic baseline-input equivalence, rather than claiming 135 additional native evaluations.

The strongest results are the missing recurrent routes in default N2048, missing nonzero-weight drive paths in whole-care6, and nearly saturated Rest motors. Graph reachability, useful signed transmission and behavior are distinct. Innate urgency and fixed biochemical conditions remain explicit qualifications. The frozen whole-care6 constructor is copied without altering historical source or assets.

The next experiment separates differential readout calibration from upstream operating-point repair before introducing recurrent drive competition. It must preserve legitimate rest, account for ATP availability with the correct sign, and avoid pretending that generic Grab is social contact or arbitrary Avoid is thermoregulation. No newly proposed weights are qualified by this baseline.
