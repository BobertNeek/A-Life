# Ordinary physiology B: eight-tick result

The frozen matrix completed once: 80 fresh CPU scenes, eight real ticks each,
640 world ticks, exit 0, seed `0xB0011108`. No early stop, host sleep interval,
host consolidation, replay mismatch, retry, extra seed or horizon extension
occurred. Body-level sleep is distinct: 11 stationary Rest rows set body.sleeping. Runtime source
`6dee4ea0` and the previously bound executable were unchanged. Exact hashes are
in `eight-tick/receipt.json`; the first-tick experiment remains a separate,
unchanged historical result.

This establishes delayed signed neural responses under real physiology, not a
finished drive controller. **The new B readout initially excites Approach.** The
bounded tonic-on arm becomes inhibitory later, while the tonic-off arm can remain
strongly excitatory. Initial branch calibration, readout calibration and circuit
weights are separate factors, not a universally successful combined prescription.
No default asset, acquired creature, GPU execution or closed study changed.

## Absolute B and the tonic circuit

B is the mean of ten existing nonnegative sensed physical features, not an
invented constant or object-label input. Its sign does not establish the sign of
`-4 B (signal - reference)`. For bounded tonic-on comfortable food, the initial
gene contribution at ticks 1–3 is **+0.190764, +0.443496, +0.326720**. It first
turns negative at tick 4 (-0.193820) and reaches -1.352730 at tick 8. The fatigued
values at ticks 1–3 are +0.190764, +0.438490, +0.289453, then -0.283015 and finally
-1.451730. The initially excitatory bias is not repaired by the delayed negative
fatigue contrast.

Bounded tonic-off B remains excitatory: tick-8 food contributions are +1.435270
comfortable and +1.706229 fatigued. Their sensed B differs after their physical
trajectories diverge. Every interval logs the actual signal, reference, B,
initial-gene B contribution and aggregate somatic readout separately. These
initial-gene contributions are not a claim that learned decoder coefficients
remain unchanged.

Source 383 is never a guaranteed constant. In this matrix its actual activation

## Actual decisions and physiology

Across 640 decisions, actual selected primitives include 283 NoLocomotion,
141 Approach, 216 Flee, 43 Rest, 62 Eat, 35 Grab and 14 Play. No Inspect primitive
is selected. There are 357 physical movements, 48 touches and 19 consumptions.
Eat selection is not equivalent to a completed meal, and Contact means Grab,
not social interaction. All neutral scenes remain physically motionless for all
eight ticks. Bounded tonic-on neutral Rest occurs at ticks 6 and 8 equally in
comfortable and fatigued scenes, so it does not demonstrate fatigue-selective
rest behavior. Only 11 of the 43 selected Rest rows actually set body.sleeping.
The other 32 have realized Locomotion movement. The production
`normalize_sleep_recovery_for_motion` rule removes sleep recovery whenever any
channel displacement is nonzero, so these 32 moving Rest choices do not realize
sleep recovery. This is an arbitration limitation under valid shared physics,
not 43 successful rests. The host reports zero sleeping residents and zero
consolidation throughout; that separate sleep lifecycle must not be confused
with body-level Rest execution.

therefore remains untested; a future separately frozen challenge should include

reproduction and unbound axes also remain outside this matrix. B2's Contact-row
ablation is a separate synthetic result and was not folded into these arms.
