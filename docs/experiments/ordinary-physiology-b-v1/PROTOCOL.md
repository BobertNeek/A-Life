## Cases and actual biology

Ten cases: neutral comfortable/fatigued; touching food comfortable/hungry/fatigued;
touching bitter food comfortable/hungry/fatigued; distant neutral object comfortable/curious.
Both signed fatigue cases are necessary to test the new B route on actual objects.
The food pair differs only in signed chemical cue +0.85/-0.85, not nutrition or
hazard. Both have nutrition 0.6 and zero hazard. The object is 2.5 units away with
zero taste. Its ordinary visible physical features, not a hidden play label,
reach the brain. The neutral scene has no target.

Newborn-only fixture chemistry sets inherited source concentrations, resolved
through each drive's actual receptor and species: hunger/fatigue/curiosity 0.1 or
0.65; fear/pain/temperature 0; loneliness 0.1; ATP 0.75; bound boredom 0.1 if present.
Reproductive drive, unbound extension and all hormones remain inherited. Body
energy is 0.8 in all cases. Cached homeostasis is rederived from the validated
biochemical graph. These are controlled initial biochemical challenges, not
claims that these concentrations are normally acquired in nature. No drive,
receptor, neural input, threshold, schedule or action is overwritten after birth.

All host steps use production perception, finalized memory, biochemical receptor
projections, `encode_cpu_frame`, actual ATP-driven microstep/route selection,
world action legality, and real physiological outcomes. Normal somatic learning
remains enabled. There are no founder optimizer updates. Candidate rows retain
innate, memory and cognitive contributions; reports separate the neural action
readout from the total logit. Forced sleep must not be called a neural Rest win.

## Bounded execution budget

1. Four unit fixtures first: counts, validated newborn chemistry/exact checkpoint,
   fixed coordinate and five-edge controls, and actual high/low ATP pricing.
   These use the existing fixture seed 7 and advance no world tick.
2. First-tick production host fixtures, only after those pass: seed 7, eight arms
   × ten cases × one tick = at most 80 world ticks. All first encoded inputs,
   candidate inputs, modulation, schedule and initial biology must match exactly
   across the neural factors. This checks physiology integration, not sustained
   behavior.
3. Proposed bounded follow-on, covered by user authority but not dispatched
   until source, binary and seed reservation are bound:
   one new seed `0xB0011108`, eight arms × ten cases × eight ticks = at most 640
   world ticks. Freeze binary/source/seed before starting. No additional seeds,
   tuning, retries after outcome inspection or early success selection. Stop each
   trial if no longer alive, health falls below 0.5 or energy below 0.05. Report
   every early stop and all unsuccessful arms.

Default invocation prints the plan only. Explicit run modes require a new receipt
directory and persist each completed case before testing cross-arm equality. A
preexisting directory is rejected, never overwritten. The two run modes cannot alter
seed, arm membership, duration or case membership. The second stage is a new
study, not a rerun of B1/B2 or a previous native association study.

## Measurements and decision criteria

Log pre/post graph-derived drives, hormones, body energy/health and position;
scalar memory source-family/action-kind/observation counts and sealed-decision
counts, weight generation and nonzero/range summaries, branch gain/threshold
summaries before/after (no private bank or learned-weight dump);
all sensed candidate features, decoder lanes, innate contributions, neural
readout and total logits; actual choices/bundles, physical success and measured
physiology; cognitive work, real ATP availability/debit, microsteps/enabled
routes; real receptor effects and regional gate offset; sources 256/259/262,
383/1967, drive relays and motor signal/reference activations. Reconstruct each
actual decision from its own replay receipt and require exact activations.

Primary signed contrasts are fatigue minus comfortable Rest in the neutral
scene, hunger minus comfortable Ingest on food and bitter controls, and curiosity
minus comfortable Inspect on the same neutral object. Compare old versus B
readout within each branch/tonic arm. Report direct neural and total-score changes
separately. More movement is not success: comfortable neutral should permit
Idle/Hold/Rest without a target or harm; movement/Grab, contact/feeding success,
energy and fatigue must be reported even when contrary to the hypothesis. Physical meals do not by themselves establish stored Ingest episodes; compare
outcomes with scalar source-family counts and the representative decision. A
short sample establishes neither learning nor a general policy. An absent sign,
saturated motor, missing opportunity, changed first input, unsolicited sleep or
failed safety bound is a result/blocker, not grounds to remove an arm.

Expand thermal, social, reproductive and unbound-extension experiments only after
these narrow physiological paths are understood. Their semantic/physics and
fresh-identity requirements are independent.
