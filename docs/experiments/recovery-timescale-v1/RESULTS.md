## Interpretation and next boundary

Rate calibration is justified by a real timescale mismatch: the unchanged
founder dies before either frozen reference can be reached, even with optimistic
full expression. Altering thresholds would confound the frozen M0/M1 routes;
increasing lifespan would hide the mismatch. Keeping retention and recovery
isolates production. Retention alone cannot solve the pressure mismatch: for
fixed baseline b and gain g with any retention in [0,1], P(t) <= b + g*t. Here
that bound is .515980011 at age 4514, still below .60 even with the conservative
rounding allowance. The closed form is ideal arithmetic with f32 coefficients,
not an exact floating-point bound; conservative rounding allowance leaves the
same impossibility conclusion. The protocol records that distinction.

A single inherited full sleep dose can clear pressure in one body interval;
this study did not redesign recovery duration or cognitive phase persistence.
A future work-linked fatigue mechanism should charge measured exertion, not
rename the Awake timer or silently penalize Idle. Future neural-policy testing
must use a separately frozen protocol and distinguish selected recovery from
these scripted physical controls. No policy study or default deployment is
implied by the body results.

Method reference: https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/
Experiments 4 (targeted), 5 (whole), 6 (iterative) inform the intervention/test process;
they provide no evidence for these biological rates.
