# Four-update matched factual curriculum: rejected

The expanded factual curriculum produced **no measured behavioral improvement**.
Untouched, saved ZeroGradient4, saved Balanced4 and new MatchedFactual4 had identical
scored physical outcomes, speech/timing and safety metrics within every matched
seed/phase. The candidate fails the predeclared requirement to improve unsupported
reporting on both fresh seeds and is **rejected, experimental and unpromoted**.
The fixed budget is complete; no additional training or simulation follows.

There was real target-specific learning in the permitted gate parameters, and
real positive care/reporting was retained. Neither establishes improved language.
[Selected results and receipts](retained-language-matched-factual-curriculum-results.json)
separate actual utterances/physical events from conditional decoder outputs.

## Gates changed, false declarations persisted

The readouts recorded during the real world decisions show a maximum first-step
presence change of **0.868755** and stop change of **0.634615** versus Untouched.
Every conditional payload, including confidence, nevertheless stays identical
to its matched Untouched payload. Those conditional outputs are distinct from
actual emitted utterances in the receipts.

For example, seed23311891's premature EatFood report before sleep changes first
presence/stop from **+0.998687/−1.004126** to **+0.401019/−0.541024**. Both remain
on the same sides of native zero thresholds. All seven actually premature
candidate reports across the paired fresh cases retain positive first presence
and negative first stop; none is suppressed. This is measured native gate
movement at real captured decisions, not a hypothetical logit fit. It rules out
a zero-update explanation here but does not establish why the objective failed
to produce useful behavior, or that a larger budget/routing change would work.

The candidate does not improve unsupported reporting against **either** saved
trained-curriculum or momentum control on **either** held-out seed. It fails the
predeclared criterion despite retaining the observed positives. Reject it and
stop at the declared four updates/704 scored decisions. No further experiment
or promotion is started.
