# Retained grounded-language CPU pilot

This opt-in experiment teaches a fresh public N2048 founder the bounded
distinction **eat food / get toy**. It runs the canonical CPU organism and world,
including biology, memory, plasticity, ATP scheduling and complete motor bundles.
It does not qualify CFF ChiChi competence or implement a living LLM harness.

```sh
cargo --config profile.test.package.alife_training.opt-level=2 \
  build -p alife_game_app --features cpu-training --profile test \
  --bin experiment_retained_language
target/debug/experiment_retained_language --disposable-public-founder \
  --output /absolute/new/directory --seed 847501 \
  --rounds 2 --epochs 16 --decisions 24
```

The output directory must be new. Training artifacts and the full compressed
native trace stay local; only source and aggregate measurements belong in Git.
The runner has no existing-creature input. Its explicit experimental reload mode
accepts only its own checksummed experimental recovery wrapper, not a joint PPO
handoff. Existing checkpoint codecs, schemas and default execution are unchanged.

## What the teacher can do

The existing bounded speech translator supplies the two literal requests. The
language nursery emits them through ordinary spatial teacher speech once every
eight world ticks. The foundation vocabulary selector supplies offline imitation
targets only for actually present native candidates. The existing teacher assembler
also labels compatible stationary motor channels. A native representative may
force manipulation or vocalization even if its conditional sampling mask is
empty; the marginal objective preserves that path. Teacher target IDs and
plans never enter the creature's encoded inputs. No action is forced during
learner capture or assessment, and no reward or world transition is synthesized.
Separate scripted world controls establish that the scene really permits
consumption with energy gain and toy acquisition; those controls are not learned
behavior.

`--llamacpp-translation` uses the existing localhost `LlamaCppSpeechTranslator`
configuration. Both confirmed provider receipts must preserve the two bounded
requests; failure stops the run without fallback. This is training-time language
translation, not a new scenario generator or a semantic-prior implementation.
The default is deterministic and makes zero real-model claims. The semantic prior
is off throughout this pilot, including training; its delivery count is explicitly
zero. No model weights or model efficacy are verified by this experiment.

## Learning and continuity

All three arms begin with identical weights and zero Adam histories. Each round
collects eight real lives, alternating food and toy goals across four layouts.
Grounded and shuffled arms receive the same offline action and speech-supervision
procedure and fixed update budget. The shuffled arm counterbalances aligned and
crossed requests by physical lesson, layout and eight-tick block. The untreated
arm receives neither teacher speech nor offline optimizer updates, but gets the
same scene seeds and simulation budget. Before capture, every subject advances
through ordinary world ticks until the native hearing, association, interaction
and vocalization developmental gates are active, remaining healthy and awake.
The measured preparation tick and maturation are retained. No age, chemical state
or development field is assigned to force readiness.

Native recurrent BPTT uses 16-decision loss windows, rebuilding each episode's
prefix under the current weights at every update. Recorded lifetime/fast offsets,
memory, routes and structural context are fixed: this is **conditional replay**,
not a reconstruction of the changing entire organism under new weights. Rows
without an actionable referent remain in replay with zero action-supervision
adjoints. Speech targets use the existing vocabulary-production gate: silence
while hearing, and a verb only after a genuine earlier successful physical event.
The actual event is retained until the first eligible silent frame, then supplies
one narration target; it is never a future or manufactured consequence. That
frame's offline action target includes the existing native Vocalize candidate.
Hearing words, loss changes, nonzero gradients and optimizer steps are reported
separately from behavior.

The trainable mask includes native recurrent, action-candidate and speech-payload
coordinates. Other coordinates and their moments/ages remain exact. Each epoch
commits one native Adam update; integer ages and both moments persist across
export/recompile. The host's prohibition on changing a living collector's founder
is preserved: learned founder weights are assessed in new cohorts. This is
retained foundation learning, not a same-individual optimizer rebase.

For both trained arms, a separately saved assessment life cold-restores in a fresh
process through the native compiler, foundation and actor/host validators. Exact
hashes cover the initial complete private life and optimizer, the next eight
native steps, the resulting private state, and the next Adam update using a
retained real gradient. That last check tests optimizer continuity; it does not
recompute that gradient in the child. No critic update or critic policy binding
is fabricated.

## Assessment and limits

A paired eight-step native probe starts from an exact shared host snapshot and
changes only the spatial request. It measures changed encoded lanes, candidate
logits and trainable gradients, with hashes of both real trajectories. It runs
before and after teaching, never updates the actor, and is separate from scored
assessment. Encoded word differences without action-objective influence are
reported as an unresolved path. A nonzero gradient difference alone still does
not demonstrate learned language.

Each arm has twelve assessment lives: two goals × two relocated layouts × three
cue conditions (ordinary player request, silence, opposite request). Teacher
actors and teacher hearing are rejected; semantic context is rejected. Player
requests are explicitly present in the request conditions, so these are not
language-free tests. Near positions reverse the trained food/toy bearings; far
positions additionally require navigation. Food, toy and a visible obstacle
distractor are checked at the initial frame. Full native failures remain in the
trace.

Physical outcomes count actual food consumption, nourishing energy gain and
ownership-changing toy grabs, not selected commands alone. Utterances count
actual world speech emissions; a correct grounded utterance must exactly match
the existing production label justified by earlier physical evidence. A zero
opportunity count does not establish failed pronunciation or successful language
learning. Request, swapped-word and silent outcomes must be compared before
claiming a language-conditioned distinction. Both goals can sometimes occur in
one life, so the counts alone do not establish selective command following.

Natural-sleep retention is not included. Any naturally occurring sleeping ticks
or consolidations during the bounded lives are reported. The small seed sample,
conditional replay and fresh-cohort boundary limit generalization. A negative
result is still a runnable assay, not a reason to relabel exposure as learning.
The two fixed phrases do not isolate noun meaning from verb meaning or establish
compositional language.

## Measured run

The initial full run is preserved in
[retained-language-initial-results.json](retained-language-initial-results.json).
It used implementation `f06826a6a55955f223a622be49136e3efd2ddd13` and completed in
386.037 seconds (external process 386.046 seconds). All three arms had zero
qualifying assessment meals, toy acquisitions and utterances. The trained actors
were identical despite receiving 96 teacher-heard frames and 32 updates each.

That initial protocol was defective: it dropped legitimate representative-forced
Eat/Get targets whenever their conditional motor mask was empty. It also started
before native hearing/association developmental gates. It is not a valid test of
the intended teaching efficacy. The corrected protocol preserves that negative
record and measures the representative path, ordinary preparation and delayed
physical narration explicitly. No hyperparameter sweep or successful seed search
was performed.

The first four-tick smoke stopped on an empty-target objective guard; its native
prefix remains local. A repaired one-update smoke completed in 90.379 seconds
with zero qualifying physical successes, and exact cold continuation for both
trained copies. It preceded the final accounting/protocol corrections and does
not contribute to final efficacy counts. Compilation, diagnostic work and those
smokes are additional costs, not included in a completed comparison's timer.
Timings describe execution cost under concurrent build/test activity, not a
performance benchmark or speedup.
