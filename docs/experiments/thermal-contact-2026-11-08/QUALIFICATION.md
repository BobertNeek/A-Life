## Combined body-withdrawal integration

The combined source incorporates the exact body component merged in main
2e13e227c6ac4ac8d883ffbaeed9f6291c6cefd0 (tree
31bfd38e1c384fd9b112bcd948f102959fecb66f), without activating either component.
The withdrawal helper and its original tests are unchanged. One integration
correction samples thermal contact at both endpoints after a physical response,
then records the final event actually passed to biology. Otherwise a withdrawal
entering heat could miss its dose, or the receipt could omit an applied dose.
This is max-of-endpoints once per interval, not two thermal doses.

A fresh generated-world fixture exercises entering and leaving contact, matched
against the same physical withdrawal without configured thermal exposure. Both
cases move backwards once on unlocalized acute damage, cancel physical recovery,
apply one thermal dose, and advance biology once. Injury and energy match the
physical-only control. A subsequent quiet interval does not repeat the reflex
merely because injury remains. No voluntary action, policy credit or cognitive
arousal is manufactured.

The combined native checks pass: 601 world tests, 15 selected core tests, no
failures, and 3 existing ignored world manual/timing benchmarks. The new focused
test is included in those 616 unique passes, not added again. Strict core/world
all-target Clippy, workspace formatting and diff whitespace checks pass.
COMBINED-QUALIFICATION.json binds source, commands, finite summaries and log hashes.

Two unsuccessful setup checks are preserved separately in the record: the first
focused test used a mathematically equivalent but bit-different f32 expression
for its expected dose, and the first core command named a nonexistent test target.
Only the expected expression and the command were corrected; production exposure

failure. These are compatibility/physical fixtures, not autonomous behavioral,
