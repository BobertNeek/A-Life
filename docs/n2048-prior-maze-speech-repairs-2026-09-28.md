## What changed

The existing terrain encoder now has separate inherited, hearing-gated routes for heard tokens and bounded private semantic associations: 128 lanes each. Token identities are symbols, never neuron addresses. N2048 retains its existing 32,768 logical weight coordinates; no CPU live neural execution was added. This changes compiler/profile identity, so recollect demonstrations and prepare a fresh warmup rather than resuming an incompatible optimizer or replay. Existing foundation weights can seed fresh compatible individuals.

The canonical live runtime now requests Qwen3.5-0.8B hints asynchronously from actual bounded perception. It supplies recently heard words, coarse needs and sensed physical properties; it supplies no hidden object locations, maze route, selected actions, weights or future outcomes. Requests are event-triggered, gain is capped at 0.2, packets expire within 32 simulation ticks, and training omits hints on approximately 20% of lives. Cache entries bind to provider/model/configuration, context and encoding identity. Replay captures the actual delivered context and encoded inputs.

At maximum simulation speed, inference can arrive after a lesson has finished. Collection setup therefore waits at most five wall seconds for the first actual-perception hint. Ordinary live ticks never wait. Private request context can retain at most six genuinely heard words for 32 ticks; this does not fabricate current hearing or write learner memory. The provider constrains JSON lengths and associations to the receiver's word palette. Receipts distinguish deliveries, failures, stale replies, priming timeouts and valid responses with no usable positive association.

The existing speech trainer now supports source-bound full single-word targets and explicit quiet targets. GPU gradients train the speech decoder and its recurrent inputs. Quiet supervision does not erase token bits. PPO continues to train motor choices; speech-token likelihood is not falsely included in its action objective. Generated phrases and token-level speech RL remain later work. The initial vocabulary is food, toy, creature, obstacle, look, approach, retreat, eat, get, rest, play and ball, with the same lexical symbols in the Hand and lessons.

Maze teaching uses a private layout-aware planner and ordinary physical turns, steps, sight, collisions and consumption. Its small route includes two deliberate dead-end visits and reversals. Food is initially visible, then opaque walls close. Layout rotation, reflection, scale and branch lengths vary; independent topology generalization remains unproven. The layout/route is never a learner input. Vocabulary lessons use ordinary spatial teacher speech and real referents. Offline imitation targets are separate from live perception; the teacher never supplies a live learner speech payload. External embodied lesson actors are not accidentally admitted as registered GPU newborns.

The teacher now holds a successfully acquired ball instead of repeatedly toggling grip/release. A get receipt requires continued possession, and releasing a ball cannot earn action praise for getting it. Eating demonstrations stop after the named meal and do not keep requesting an already consumed target. Praise remains a bounded ordinary world event with a cooldown, not an evaluator-authored reward scalar.

World objects now include three food recipes and two real toys through the existing registry and executors. Root, fruit and seed differ in nutrition, appearance and chemical cues; contact taste is not exposed at a distance. A ball supports existing carry/release. A fixed activity toy rejects grabbing. Play is an ordinary contact activation attempt, available without revealing a hidden desirable target; the world measures success before emitting stimulation. New founders inherit a separate boredom circuit through both biochemical homologs. Emission, receptors and biological valuation are gene-controlled. Loaded genomes are not silently rewritten. The renderer projects actual object properties; final art, ball rolling and a GUI playtest are not claimed.

Shared category words, narrower words and tracked identities can support similarity without equating objects. A new object inviting curious investigation, then familiar toys relieving boredom through repeat play, is the user's intended learned progression. It has **not** been demonstrated or implemented as a hardcoded interaction-count switch. Generalization to unseen variants is **Unknown**.
