This report covers the seven gaps following the September 28 prior/maze/speech repair. It supersedes that report's remaining-work list, not its historical receipts. No overnight campaign or founder promotion is authorized by this repair.

## Changed behavior

1. Teacher-free collection now primes the private Qwen prior after the initial physical cue and before its first neural decision. The bounded setup wait does not advance biological time. Ordinary gameplay remains asynchronous.
2. Production speech targets describe a real bodily need or a successful previous physical action. Hearing alone does not earn an echo target. The existing GPU speech decoder learns recurrent short phrases, including their termination, without pretending token likelihood is part of motor PPO.
3. Seeded maze spanning trees change corridor connectivity, junctions and dead ends. The private planner demonstrates turns, steps and backtracking through world-owned collisions; its route is never a learner input. Food is seen before opaque walls close.
4. Navigation, understanding and speaking receive separate frozen behavior scores. Periodic disjoint-seed panels hold or advance the curriculum; a small paired prior-off screen must also pass before advancement. Language checkpoints are no longer ranked by energy or meals. These small progression screens are not final acceptance or promotion.
5. Familiarity derives from retained inspection/interaction/grasp memories through the existing bounded object index. Novelty stimulates inherited curiosity chemistry; successful investigation relieves it. Each channel uses the familiarity of its actual interaction target, so looking at a familiar object cannot make playing with a different new toy relieve boredom. Familiar toys increasingly stimulate inherited boredom relief. No parallel interaction counter or fixed switch was added, and old saved genomes are not silently rewritten.
6. The shared vocabulary has eighteen words, with root/fruit/seed, ball/activity and hungry/tired distinctions. Lessons include competing examples, physical noun/verb associations and short grounded phrases. Bodily need lessons use normal biological preconditioning rather than chemical injection. Speaking and understanding have equal representation.
7. Preparation uses the same release collector, GPU trainer and sealed optimizer handoff as the campaign. Exact checkpoint state also preserves the private prior's fading controller and recent context. Save validators now permit external physical teachers alongside registered creatures, matching the live-world contract while retaining unique identities and complete registered bindings. Their IDs are reserved at spawn, preventing save normalization or later births from changing/reusing them. External teachers do not acquire a registered learner brain.

The default Qwen launcher now selects the acquired Qwen3.5-0.8B Q8_0 model, rather than the old larger model. The collector defaults to 86 demonstrations; `-ExamplesPerWord 1` creates a bounded 50-demonstration preparation corpus covering all eighteen words in both directions.
