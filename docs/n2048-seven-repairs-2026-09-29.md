These small progression screens are not final acceptance or promotion.
5. Familiarity derives from retained inspection/interaction/grasp memories through the existing bounded object index. Novelty stimulates inherited curiosity chemistry; successful investigation relieves it. Familiar toys increasingly stimulate inherited boredom relief. No parallel interaction counter or fixed switch was added, and old saved genomes are not silently rewritten.
6. The shared vocabulary has eighteen words, with root/fruit/seed, ball/activity and hungry/tired distinctions. Lessons include competing examples, physical noun/verb associations and short grounded phrases. Bodily need lessons use normal biological preconditioning rather than chemical injection. Speaking and understanding have equal representation.

Exact checkpoint state also preserves the private prior's fading controller and recent context.
