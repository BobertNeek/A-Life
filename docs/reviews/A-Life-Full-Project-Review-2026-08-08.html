The simulation and the game miss each other

The GPU runtime mutates a real HeadlessWorld. The renderer constructs creature transforms from persisted tiles and applies procedural pose offsets. The returned live tick summaries are discarded.

What still prevents the game

Autonomous life is only partly on the main tick

HeadlessWorld::advance_tick advances metabolism, upkeep, speech, resource ecology and terminality. The live runtime archives and removes dead residents; the production Bevy tick drains their stable IDs, removes entity-map bindings, despawns mapped creature roots, and repairs or clears selection, camera and inspector focus. Reproduction remains.

Unified state is source-complete, not runtime-proven

The world record now owns biological identity across action, save and GPU resident restore paths. Checkpoint cognition remains separate by design. The newest restore slice passed independent static review, but its compiler and playable-runtime status remains UNKNOWN after earlier bounded timeouts.

Runtime truth is joined; the visible control receipt is still thin

Pause, single-step and speed govern the authoritative scheduler. Durable load now replaces the canonical world and live GPU state atomically, and managed breeding creates an archived GPU-resident child. The remaining gap is one real input-and-screen receipt for these controls, not another runtime authority.

Learning is causally proven once and now has a truthful compact explanation

The integrated loop proves a different post-sleep action and joint outcome with real memory, prediction, topology and learning receipts. The selected-creature inspector now compares previous/current actions and measured outcomes, sleep work and update counts. It deliberately does not invent a hidden reason; on-screen capture remains pending.

Scale is designed, not demonstrated

N2048 and 500-hot-brain budgets exist. Product loops cap at 8 or 30 in places. Per-tick CPU preparation and synchronous GPU wait/map remain likely bottlenecks.

Docs describe several eras at once

Older CPU-fallback and headless-only text coexists with newer no-fallback GPU authority and a production voxel path. “Implemented Era 1” can be misread as “promoted Era 1,” though the report is blocked.
