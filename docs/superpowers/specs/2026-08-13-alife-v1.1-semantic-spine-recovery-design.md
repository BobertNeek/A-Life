# A-Life v1.1 Full Architecture Recovery Design

> **SUPERSEDED / HISTORICAL:** This recovery design records the former v1.1
> program. The v2.0 controlling architecture now governs all future work. Do
> not execute this design as current authority.

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** Superseded historical recovery design
**Former controlling architecture:** `docs/brain/ALife_Adaptive_Brain_Architecture_Spec_v1.1.md`
**Delivery strategy:** broad rough implementation first; deep repair, validation, and optimization second

## 1. Objective

Recover the intended A-Life architecture as one causal game rather than polish a narrow subset.

Pass 1 will attempt every major locked v1.1 capability with the simplest real mechanism that satisfies its semantic contract. Each mechanism must participate in cognition, embodiment, learning, development, lifecycle, persistence, or evolution. A type, serializer, diagnostic, placeholder return, disconnected sidecar, or future-facing interface does not count.

The approximate one-day preference is pace pressure, not a deadline and not permission to narrow the architecture. Pass 1 may be rough, naive, inefficient, or ugly. It may need substantial repair in Pass 2. It must still be real, causally integrated, and green under its small set of focused tests.

## 2. Delivery rule

The governing trade is:

> Reduce verification depth, not implementation breadth or correctness.

Pass 1 therefore favors a simple working implementation of the complete intended architecture over either a polished subset or a partially represented architecture.

The work advances across the full dependency chain. Once a mechanism basically works, participates causally, compiles, and passes its focused gate, work moves to the next missing capability. Pass 1 does not spend hours tuning one subsystem while other major v1.1 capabilities remain absent.

Pass 1 does not knowingly leave relevant tests failing. It does avoid exhaustive edge cases, broad matrices, long soaks, statistical confidence runs, large corpora, ablations, exhaustive evidence generation, premature population scaling, and deep optimization.

Pass 2 begins only after the broad architecture is present. It performs the deeper review, repair, profiling, tuning, behavioral validation, population scaling, robustness work, cleanup, and release evidence.

## 3. Architectural authority

The authority order is:

1. the user's goal: smart, fast, compute-efficient, evolvable, biology-inspired organisms;
2. v1.1 locked goals, capabilities, invariants, and semantic interfaces;
3. explicit architectural decisions and approved mechanism substitutions;
4. current Rust types, GPU layouts, tests, constants, fixtures, adapters, processor placement, and implementation structure.

Current code is a migration baseline. It is not evidence of intended architecture merely because it exists. N2048, one world configuration, one hardware target, one phenotype, a current test fixture, or a temporary implementation limit must not silently become a rule for all future organisms.

Reference mechanisms are replaceable when a substitute preserves the locked capability, invariants, and interfaces and is equal or better in capability per compute. If implementation work reveals a better design, it becomes an explicit architectural decision. It does not quietly redefine v1.1.

Biological mechanisms are useful when they improve capability, efficiency, robustness, development, or evolvability. Biological resemblance alone is not a completion criterion. Efficiency work is welcome, but it may not amputate cognition merely to simplify implementation.

## 4. Current baseline and protected work

The current committed source already contains more integration than the 2026-08-08 review recorded:

- the production schedule can run GPU ticks;
- the world owns organism records, biology, lifecycle, actions, and outcomes;
- stable-ID presentation frames are published after successful ticks;
- existing creature transforms can consume authoritative positions;
- conception, birth, ageing, death, archive ordering, pause/speed scheduling, and runtime replacement helpers exist.

The remaining architectural breaks include:

- newborn GPU residents can be compiled from a global template rather than their inherited record;
- cognition can advance mutable homeostasis and development separately from world biology;
- the implemented brain selects one semantic action instead of a factorized command bundle;
- concepts and unresolved gaps are largely diagnostic and post-decision;
- prediction lacks a grounded non-collapsing target contract;
- structural growth, dendritic conjunction, integrated sleep, and cross-system cognitive work accounting are incomplete;
- birth, retirement, appearance, selection, following, inspection, and load do not yet form one reliable reconciliation path;
- some tests prove plumbing or internal layouts rather than production capability.

The existing dirty `AGENTS.md` and uncommitted `production_voxel_renderer.rs` change are protected user work. The renderer change must be reviewed by capability. Useful atomic-load work may be retained. Any regression of committed birth reconciliation must be repaired. It is neither accepted nor discarded wholesale.

## 5. Pass 1 architecture

### 5.1 One authoritative organism

The simulation must maintain one canonical organism-state record. The current recovery implementation uses `WorldOrganismRecord` for that role. The record's architectural responsibility covers:

- stable organism, world, lineage, archive, and lifecycle identity;
- inherited genome and expressed phenotype;
- position, orientation, body, and morphology-relevant state;
- homeostasis, chemistry, endocrine state, fatigue, injury, and sleep pressure;
- age, development, growth, reproduction, birth, and death.

GPU-resident state owns neural execution state:

- activation and recurrent state;
- foundation, genetic, lifetime, and fast weight banks;
- eligibility transactions and learning state;
- sparse neural layouts, branch state, and packed offsets;
- bounded decoder, prediction, and cognitive diagnostics.

Each tick derives an immutable versioned cognitive input from the authoritative organism record. A GPU cache may exist only when it is digest-bound to that record and replaced atomically. It may not advance a second biological truth.

Birth and load use the same record-derived admission route. Ordinary offspring compile from their actual inherited genome and expressed phenotype. No global-template shortcut controls ordinary development.

### 5.2 Integrated causal scheduler

The production loop is:

1. Read authoritative organisms, bodies, biology, and world state.
2. Build cheap peripheral perception and bounded affordance hints without host desirability scores.
3. Retrieve bounded memory, active concepts, causal relations, uncertainty, and unresolved gaps.
4. Allocate focal attention within the organism's budget.
5. Assemble one immutable `CognitiveContextFrame`.
6. Run due recurrent, predictive, dendritic, and motor computation.
7. Decode a factorized `MotorCommandBundle`.
8. Let the world validate and execute the joint command.
9. Advance each due biological and world subsystem exactly once according to its configured cadence for the current causal sequence.
10. Observe the successor state and seal one exact `ExperiencePatch`.
11. Apply eligible neural learning, prediction learning, memory, concept and gap updates, structural evidence, cost, and sleep queues exactly once.
12. Publish an immutable stable-ID presentation snapshot.
13. Reconcile body, animation, labels, selection, follow camera, inspectors, birth, and retirement.

Pipelining is permitted only when sequence, organism identity, source digest, action identity, and outcome identity remain exact.

### 5.3 Attention

Pass 1 implements a simple two-tier attention mechanism:

- a cheap peripheral summary over available sensory channels;
- a bounded focal set chosen by top-K salience;
- hysteresis so focus does not churn without evidence;
- heritable or learnable channel weights within bounded limits;
- modulation from body needs, memory expectancy, active concepts, novelty, uncertainty, and unresolved gaps;
- explicit attention and cognitive-work budgets.

The host may provide legality and bounded affordance information. It may not choose what is desirable for the organism. The resulting focal selection must change the neural context and therefore be capable of changing prediction or behavior.

### 5.4 Active concepts and unresolved gaps

Concept induction and neural structural growth remain distinct systems.

Pass 1 concepts use bounded evidence summaries and utility estimates. They can activate, strengthen, decay, split, merge, and retire under simple thresholds. Active concepts enter `CognitiveContextFrame` and can modulate attention, neural context, prediction, or behavior.

`UnresolvedGap` records a bounded mismatch, missing relation, or persistent uncertainty. Gaps influence pre-decision attention or neural context and can therefore alter later prediction or behavior. A diagnostic-only gap list does not count.

Concept relations may supply bounded evidence to structural growth. They do not automatically become synapses, and a concept edge is never treated as a neural edge by identity.

### 5.5 Non-collapsing predictive learning

Pass 1 implements action-conditioned prediction against grounded successor observations. The target receipt includes the exact organism, context, command bundle, world sequence, successor observation, and source digest.

The objective must preserve information. The simplest acceptable implementation predicts a bounded normalized feature vector drawn from authoritative next-state observations and uses at least one anti-collapse constraint, such as fixed target projections plus variance or covariance floors. A predictor that can minimize loss by emitting one constant is not acceptable.

Prediction error may train the predictor and enter bounded neural or concept context. It is not fabricated reward. It may influence curiosity or attention only through an explicit bounded route.

### 5.6 Factorized motor control

The production cognitive ABI becomes a bounded `MotorCommandBundle` with individual locomotion, orientation, manipulation, vocal, posture, and optional species-specific channels.

Pass 1 may map these channels onto existing world actions through an explicit compatibility adapter. The decision receipt preserves every selected channel. The world executes one joint physical command and produces one joint causal outcome. Per-channel observations are recorded only when the world can measure them. The implementation never fabricates per-channel rewards from a joint outcome.

The former single semantic action can remain behind the adapter for migration. It cannot remain the controlling cognitive interface.

### 5.7 Dendritic conjunctions

Pass 1 gives each supported neuron a small bounded set of flat branch descriptors. Each branch combines a sparse list of inputs with a simple nonlinear gate. Branch output changes the neuron's computation before the final activation.

Exact branch counts, placement, nonlinearities, and inheritance details remain tunable. A serialized descriptor that never affects activation does not count.

### 5.8 Sparse structural plasticity and synaptogenesis

Pass 1 implements actual bounded sparse growth and pruning.

Candidate discovery uses local event sketches, co-activation traces, eligibility neighborhoods, concept or gap evidence summaries, or other sparse bounded sources. It must not scan all neuron pairs. A bounded top-K candidate store accumulates evidence. Sleep or another explicit safe structural phase accepts a small number of candidates, prunes weak edges, preserves total structural budgets, and rebuilds affected sparse layouts atomically.

Growth must change later neural computation. An interface for future growth, a counter, or pruning without growth does not satisfy this capability.

### 5.9 Integrated sleep and consolidation

Sleep is a scheduled organism state, not a memory utility call. Pass 1 implements a simple phase sequence that can:

- replay bounded experiences;
- consolidate fast learning into longer-lived weights under policy;
- update predictive and contextual representations;
- consolidate or retire concepts and unresolved gaps;
- apply bounded structural growth and pruning;
- update sleep pressure and wake state through authoritative biology.

Sleep competes with waking time and energy. Its work is measured. Later waking cognition must be capable of changing because sleep occurred.

### 5.10 Cognition, body, biology, lifecycle, and ecology

Metabolism, resource consumption, growth, ageing, injury, health, sleep pressure, reproduction, birth, death, and ecological turnover share the deterministic causal scheduler with cognition. Each subsystem advances only when due under its own configured cadence.

The brain reads authoritative interoception and issues motor commands. The world owns legality and consequences. The sealed successor receipt trains cognition. Reproduction passes genetic architecture and developmental parameters through the actual genome. Lifetime learning is not inherited unless the controlling architecture explicitly permits a separate mechanism.

A small deterministic development world is acceptable for fast smoke tests. This does not create a one-creature architecture. All contracts remain keyed by stable organism identity and use bounded batch-shaped storage so population growth changes capacity and scheduling rather than authority.

### 5.11 Cognitive work accounting

Pass 1 must measure deterministic hardware-independent cognitive work units across major subsystems, including recurrent updates, attention, prediction, memory retrieval and write, concept and gap work, dendritic branches, structural candidate work, learning, and sleep consolidation.

A world or species policy may convert cognitive work into biological energy or metabolic cost. Ecology and evolution scenarios should normally enable that policy. Debugging worlds and behavioral benchmarks may disable it to avoid confounding cognition with starvation. When enabled, the conversion must be deterministic, recorded, and applied to authoritative biology. GPU time alone is not the semantic cost unit.

### 5.12 Development and evolution

The ordinary genome and phenotype route exposes bounded heritable parameters needed by attention, concept formation, prediction, motor decoding, dendritic capacity, structural growth, sleep, learning rates, and cognitive economics.

Development constructs the expressed brain from those parameters. Reproduction mutates and recombines them through existing bounded genetic mechanisms. Larger brains and more work remain possible evolutionary outcomes, not fixed assumptions or free upgrades.

Pass 1 does not claim evolved intelligence. It restores the ability for selection to act on the intended architecture.

### 5.13 Presentation and controls

Presentation consumes immutable snapshots keyed by stable organism and world identity. A snapshot carries enough authoritative state to project:

- position and orientation;
- lifecycle presence;
- phenotype and body appearance;
- health, injury, sleep, age, and reproductive state needed by current visuals;
- selected motor channels and measured outcome summaries for animation and inspection;
- lineage and archive identity.

One reconciler performs spawn, update, selection, following, inspection, and retirement. A newborn body is built from its phenotype, never by cloning an arbitrary root. Every rendered root is bound into the same stable-ID map used by readers and retirement.

Pause and speed govern authoritative ticks. School and managed-breeding actions execute through the same world authority and display measured outcomes.

### 5.14 Persistence

Save checkpoints the authoritative world, organisms, brain execution and learning state, cognitive context stores, structural state, scheduler sequence, control state, and stable presentation identities needed for exact resume.

Load builds and validates a complete candidate world, runtime, and presentation binding, then atomically replaces current state. Failure leaves current state unchanged. Durable founder or archive formats remain explicit, versioned transformations rather than pretending to be exact runtime checkpoints.

## 6. Pass 1 verification strategy

Pass 1 keeps the repository green with the smallest tests that can reveal semantic failure.

The default loop for a coherent implementation batch is:

1. edit the smallest owned file set;
2. run formatting or a compile/check once;
3. run one focused capability test or one short causal smoke;
4. fix failures before moving on;
5. do not repeat successful checks unless relevant state changed.

High-value gates may combine related mechanisms. The minimum proof set is:

1. **Authority and lifecycle:** newborn and loaded residents derive genome, phenotype, interoception, and development from their matching world records; spawn and retirement reconcile by stable identity.
2. **Production causal loop:** a real selected motor bundle changes the authoritative world and matching presentation entity through the installed schedule, without manual presentation injection.
3. **Attention, concepts, and gaps:** a grounded change in memory, concept, or gap context changes focal allocation or neural context before decision.
4. **Prediction:** distinct grounded successor states cannot be satisfied by one constant prediction, and prediction learning changes a later prediction.
5. **Motor factorization:** individual channels survive decode and sealing; the world records one joint physical outcome without invented per-channel reward.
6. **Dendritic and structural computation:** a conjunction affects activation, and one sparse accepted growth candidate changes later computation without an all-pairs scan.
7. **Sleep:** a short wake-sleep-wake scenario changes a later memory, predictor, weight, concept, structure, or decision through the installed sleep phases.
8. **Cognitive economics:** every tick produces a deterministic hardware-independent work receipt; an enabled world or species policy converts it into authoritative biological cost, while a disabled policy leaves energy unchanged.
9. **Persistence:** exact checkpoint load replaces the full runtime atomically; rejected load preserves it.
10. **Short game scenario:** observe, attend, predict, act, receive an outcome, learn, sleep, act differently later, reproduce, and inspect lineage in a small deterministic world.

These gates should use the shortest viable runtime and smallest brain class that exercises the real path. GPU execution is used only where GPU causality is the claim. Pass 1 does not run broad workspaces, full matrices, long soaks, EI corpora, population benchmarks, or redundant variants after every change.

Tests that freeze private names, exact internal vectors, fixed delays, candidate indices, or N2048 layouts remain only when they are explicit versioned compatibility contracts. Otherwise they are rewritten around capability or removed.

## 7. Pass 1 execution shape

Implementation proceeds in dependency order but does not stop after a vertical slice:

1. install v1.1 authority and compliance status;
2. repair organism authority, birth, load, and the scheduler spine;
3. install the semantic contracts for context, attention, prediction, motor bundles, structural evidence, sleep, and cost;
4. add attention, concepts, and unresolved gaps to pre-decision cognition;
5. add grounded prediction and factorized motor control;
6. add dendritic conjunction and sparse structural growth;
7. integrate sleep, cognitive economics, development, and evolution;
8. complete presentation, controls, lifecycle reconciliation, and persistence;
9. run the short integrated scenario and repair obvious cross-system faults.

Small scoped workers may implement independent files or mechanisms. Cargo and GPU commands remain serialized. Each worktree is removed immediately after its accepted commit is integrated. The supervisor owns architectural decisions, integration order, canonical documentation, and protection of unrelated work.

## 8. Cleanup and replacement rule

Competing or apparently unused systems may be removed when:

1. their intended capability is named;
2. the surviving authority and real mechanism are named;
3. unique behavior and durable data are migrated;
4. no live caller or v1.1 capability depends on them;
5. the focused causal gate passes without them;
6. deletion is independently reviewable and recoverable through Git.

Duplicate biological authorities, obsolete adapters, disconnected sidecars, source-text tests, dead caches, and superseded whole-body policy paths are expendable. Apparently unused systems that represent a deferred architectural capability are not deleted until that capability is recovered or explicitly superseded.

## 9. Pass 1 completion gate

Pass 1 is complete only when all major locked v1.1 capabilities named above have a simple real implementation that participates causally in the production architecture.

The integrated system must be able to:

- perceive authoritative body and world state;
- allocate attention using bounded internal context;
- activate, update, and use concepts and unresolved gaps;
- predict grounded successor state with a non-collapsing objective;
- issue and preserve factorized motor commands;
- execute one joint physical outcome through world authority;
- learn from the exact successor receipt;
- use dendritic conjunctions in computation;
- grow and prune sparse neural structure from non-quadratic candidates;
- consolidate during biologically scheduled sleep;
- emit a deterministic hardware-independent cognitive-work receipt and apply biological cost only when world or species policy enables it;
- develop, reproduce, die, and archive through one lifecycle;
- save and atomically restore the same world and cognition;
- update the same rendered creature, body, selection, and inspector by stable identity.

No required subsystem may remain a stub, placeholder, disconnected diagnostic, or TODO-only representation. Existing relevant tests and the focused Pass 1 gates must be green.

## 10. Pass 2

Pass 2 performs the work deliberately minimized in Pass 1:

- broad regression and edge-case testing;
- long-run stability and save compatibility;
- behavioral validation and causal evidence capture;
- prediction, attention, concept, sleep, and structural ablations;
- EI ability diagnosis and fresh current-source corpora;
- CPU, GPU, memory, asynchronous readback, and cognitive-work profiling;
- tuning, cleanup, and removal of temporary compatibility adapters;
- population scaling at 30, 50, 100, 250, and 500 residents;
- robustness across brain classes, phenotypes, worlds, and hardware targets;
- alpha packaging and release evidence.

Pass 2 may repair any rough mechanism exposed by broad integration. It may optimize or replace reference mechanisms. It may not narrow a locked capability to make validation easier.

## 11. Explicit boundary

The v1.1 feature classified by the former controlling specification as deferred planning was deferred under that historical plan. Optional semantic priors could remain disabled when no real model was available, provided the bounded interface and baseline behavior were honest.

Everything else named as a major locked v1.1 capability is attempted in Pass 1. A blocker is reported as a blocker. It is not silently converted into a smaller architecture.
