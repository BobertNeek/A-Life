# Care-first next-run plan — 2026-10-19

This is an operator plan for the approved gameplay, not a training receipt or
architecture change. Training and learning evaluations remain on hold until the
mechanics, care controls, and source-bound checks are ready. The next authorized
execution should begin with a brief silent care baseline and advance when useful
care is already competent, rather than repeat a qualification campaign.

## Preserve the accepted source

Keep policy7400, its foundation, actor/value state, full Adam state and ages,
original receipts, seed ledger, and continuity audit unchanged. Any future
continuation must admit the exact sealed source package and record its executable,
world signature, and policy identity. A source-only asset evaluation must not be
reported as restoration of a complete terminal individual. Newly constructed
episodic worlds retain the admitted policy lineage; they do not establish
familiarity, durable personal memory, or uninterrupted individual continuity.

## Sequence

1. Retain held-food care with `eat_held_food`: a naturally hungry, healthy learner
   already holds the exact safe target. Success requires that learner's selected
   Eat to consume it and produce measured nourishing physiology.
2. Use `eat_reachable_food` for naturally needed, legally reachable unheld food.
   The first selected Eat acquires the target, with zero nutrition. A later Eat
   must consume and nourish. This scene has no explicit-Grab acquisition bonus.
3. Add a short approach to a currently visible food target using Feeding. Built-in
   local clearance handles nearby obstacles; the policy learns destination choice,
   continuation, and actual eating. Do not train the actuator's local steering as
   a separate prerequisite.
4. Introduce visible food/toy and hungry/tired choices, preserving ordinary needs
   and measured consequences. Real play follows physical preparation; pickup and
   placement do not count as completed Play or earn activity praise.
5. Only after useful visible care, introduce hidden intermediate routes. Hidden
   corners require learned intermediate movement and renewed observation; a
   remembered destination alone never activates automatic corner avoidance.

Language naming and short spatial requests accompany early care. Literal speech
production and perfect word matching do not block eating progression. Deliberate
Grab remains useful for chosen carrying; it is not a standalone care prerequisite.
The generic VisionLanguage 40/40/20 schedule is not the next-run curriculum.

## Distinguish prompted and silent evidence

The existing `--evaluate` command accepts `--silent-request` for held-food,
reachable-food, and Feeding care. The API uses
`FoundationEvaluationConfig.silent_request`. The default is prompted. Silent care
suppresses recurring spatial requests, including the initial request and the
24-tick repeats. Keep outputs separate and retain each receipt's `teacher_mode`,
`silent_request`, configured cue tokens, actual cue frames, source identity, and
sampling configuration. A missing historical silence flag is not evidence that
an earlier probe was silent.

These evaluations have no demonstrator or offline optimizer update. Ordinary
in-world lifetime learning remains active; report that boundary explicitly.
A brief baseline should compare useful care, not establish broad generalization
or retrain an already competent behavior. No baseline is launched by this plan.

## Acceptance and reporting

Use `FoundationFoodOutcome` for the exact learner/target ownership, consumption,
and `nourished_meal_events`. Report ordinary acquisition separately from explicit
Grab and from nourishment. The Grab experiment's +0.25 acquisition credit is not
nutrition and does not enter automatic care pickup. Report blocked actions as a
separate diagnostic; an earlier bump, or another motor channel's failure, must not
invalidate a genuine exact-target nourishing meal.

Vocabulary action counts and vocabulary lesson completion measure word/action
selection, not completed care. Successful play praise requires the matching
completed physical activation receipt; selecting PLAY against a missing affordance
does not qualify. Preserve ordinary novel-toy investigation and curiosity: novelty
can reduce the stimulation dose to zero without authorizing any biology change.

Before stage 4, add a need-choice assessment of exact-target Play activation and
measured boredom relief when bored. A novelty investigation is separate evidence,
not proof of boredom relief. Recovery already has ordinary fatigue reduction and
re-engagement; its later care assessment must use executed posture and measured
physiology and accept interrupted or factorized recovery. The current Recovery
lesson's representative-candidate and strict Rest-prefix predicates are a known
assessment limitation, not a care competence gate for stages 1-3. These later-stage
assessment checks are deferred until that stage is authorized; no new biology or
general assessment framework is required for immediate held/reachable-food care.

Software checks establish the execution and assessment contracts only. They do
not establish current policy competence, founder promotion, durable memory, or
permission to begin training. Once implementation checks pass, report readiness
and remaining source/admission limitations to the operator before execution.
