# Gullglass Head

The native east coast falls almost twenty metres over some ten-metre horizontal runs. Top-down island paint stretches over that steep face. This study keys its mineral art to vertical elevation rather than covering the terrain with another layer of flat stone meshes. Step onto the low original stone at x409.6,z−97 (about +0.21 m, with a local grade near 0.20) to look along the water, or view the whole true headland from offshore. Neither camera proves that a creature walked there. The thirteen archived native roots are retained in place; the sea, skyline, shader inputs and lights are held the same in the two sides.

A horizontal original-V1 gate limits rock to the eastward exposed surface, using real normals, native height above mean water, and smooth ends. The vertical world-addressed pigment carries interrupted pale mineral fabric, small differently aged fractures, occasional iron-oxide and lichen, and a nonuniform lower spray margin. The accompanying elevation-space height image gives very shallow bump, not displacement. The ground mesh itself provides the ledge, submerged approach and skyline at every level; the proposal adds zero stone geometry and invents no island, cove, wave, tide line, pool or route. The native colour and ripple images are read back by Git blob SHA. The photographs use both exact source textures at their documented metre scales; the offline Blender material is not a measurement or proof of identical rendering in Bevy.

Reproduce in a disposable artist directory only: run profile.py NATIVE SHELF OUT; face.py NATIVE OUT; layout.py NATIVE OUT/scene. The held shelf folder contains shore.png, ripple.png and the physical V1 water.json receipt. The other static inputs are those recorded with Lanternback Rim at 3de1d24c. With local Blender, photo.py -- NATIVE OUT/scene SHELF SUNSPINDLE-GALLERY OUT PHOTOS pairs creates the locked 840 × 500 frames. The portable desk is then desk.py -- NATIVE SHELF OUT PHOTOS; both camera transforms and all side-to-side image hashes travel with it. Brush colour, relief, angle mask and stock water remain separate editable surfaces. This is a source candidate, not a native build or registered patch.

The first full-frame paint at 2a8723a looked like a striped curtain in the offshore picture. It is intentionally not the selected pigment. The selected pass only lets that bedding surface in three separated, softly edged mineral lenses; the rest of the mass is irregular non-directional stone, fine intersecting joints and broken rain weathering. The separate 1.51 m mica colour/height tile supplies legible centimetre-scale grain without requiring stripes to do that job. No macro geometry, actor or camera was altered to dodge the problem. The rejected stripe-heavy right images do not appear in the gallery.
