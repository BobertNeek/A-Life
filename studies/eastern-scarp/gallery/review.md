# Gullglass Head

The original V1 sea cliff is a largely smooth dark cut. At the same offshore view the selected art is quieter than the rejected full-width stripe treatment: it picks out a few iron-warmed masses and damp stone, with no repeating ribs all the way along the face. The low waterline camera is backlit: the near surface changes in colour and grain, but at this distance its relief is still modest, and it does not yet supply the strong close scale cue that a true foreground object would. Keep this as the underlying material and real-sea lighting study, not a claim that the final coastal art is done. Both pictures use the independently matched stock optical and ripple maps, and neither is gameplay.

Static Blender material photography. Actual V1 heights and stock sea maps; not native rendering, navigation, frame timing or a change to saves/physics.
