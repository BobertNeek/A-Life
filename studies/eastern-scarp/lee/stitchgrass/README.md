# Stitchgrass over Twin Needle Rise

The paired spruce were readable, but the original close desk showed metre after metre of empty slope between widely spaced shrubs. This editable meadow study adds a much lower layer that *belongs to this ground*: four dimorphic, wind-combed fans in twelve broken swaths along the actual sheltered grade. Slender amber seed scales sit off a real upright culm; irregular low olive leaves bend into the wind without making a radial knot. They grow in 131 discontinuous rhizomes, rather than a regularly planted row or a radial grass brush. All 590 shoot coordinates and their true native planting-plane gradients are archived; old shrubs retain their places, and no new food, cover, walkability, collision, physics, save or default is defined.

The near scene uses 122 folded shoots and 468 simpler shoots (73,298 placed plant triangles). The separate guardian state switches all 590 shoots to four ten-triangle shapes (5,900 placed triangles). The far shapes trace each exact detailed plant’s tallest seed, downwind leaf and both lateral tips rather than shrinking the whole silhouette. These are mutually exclusive, static artist LOD choices, not an engine benchmark. A grounded 18.75 × 12.5 m section of the slope receives a 1.95 cm/texel pigment transfer: traces of fallen ochre blades and dark organic seams respond to the actual grass pockets without hiding the original substrate. The close kit also includes only 4,628 true-ground litter triangles: 341 fallen blades, 491 split hulls and 260 flecks of grit. Every tiny basal vertex samples the actual V1 triangle underneath it; none is pasted to a flat plane. The guardian does not draw these. The kit files are independently reusable: no original land, water or tree meshes are duplicated inside them.

There are **952 real V1 surface triangles**, plus 180 vertical 20 m streaming-skirt faces in the archived 1,132-face native *render primitive*. Those skirts previously showed up as strips dangling beneath the open-sided model. This artist desk removes only those drawing aids, and clips its own sea sheet against the actual submerged native triangles. No native chunk, vertex, coast or tree was changed. The unchanged older desk is preserved at fda268b2.

**Opening the edit.** The four glTF scenes are held Hollow, held Rise, proposed close meadow, proposed guardian meadow. For a direct A/B use scene 1 before; scene 2 after for the two body-height views; scene 3 after for the guardian. Camera 0 looks uphill at the actual twin crowns from the original 1.04 m eye, camera 1 is the 129 m orthographic section, and camera 2 looks toward the nearest existing willow from the very same eye. Colour desk illustrations use the actual GLB triangles/instances, linear filtered UVs, original crown alpha cutoff and a simple matte directional light. Their grey surround is outside the crop. The optional --contact-light comparison uses local directional shadows cast by real source triangles, not painted contact disks. No sky, native timing, gameplay frame or Blender execution is implied.

**Author.** `plan.py NATIVE HOLLOW RISE OUTPUT` writes the registered plants. `kit.py EARLIER-TWIN-NEEDLE.glb OUTPUT` exports the four-scene edit, the mutually exclusive eye and guardian plant-only GLBs and a neutral-edge thatch transfer. `material.py` is the optional Blender-only binder for the existing full habitat setup: apply after the held Rise ground coat and load exactly one plant-only file. It is authoring source, not a renderer registration.

**Full habitat photography (prepared, not yet executed).** Use `atelier.py` inside the original Blender installation with `-- NATIVE SCENE SHELF HOLLOW HEADLAND CUPS UPPER TOE WASH LACE RISE WOVEN OUTPUT`. The prepared stage retains the real outer native terrain, original mapped surrounding trees, previous coast, original marine sky and water on *both* sides. It imports the respective plant kit and exact fine ground transfer only on the revised side. It makes the two matched near cameras and one guardian. Do not substitute the neutral GLB desk for this or for a later in-game capture.


**Practical artist handoff.** The four-scene GLB is the editor's folio, not a shipping instancer. `mounting.py RISE WOVEN OUTPUT` emits a **455,208-byte, 30-mesh local specimen GLB** and the one canonical register for these 13 form families. Pick one mesh and scale per form/distance. Its 771 authored roots (181 earlier Rise + 590 stitchgrass) occupy 36 eight-metre cells. Do **not** separately load the earlier Rise roots, either posed stitchgrass kit, or re-add the 539 held Hollow plants. The latter belong to the old habitat, not this planting register. Each pose preserves the authored root to 0.00001 m, with glTF quaternion and separate close/mid/far scales. No pacing, in-game popping, performance or native adoption has been measured here. The real field and save state stay as they were.
