# Stitchgrass over Twin Needle Rise

The paired spruce were readable, but the original close desk showed metre after metre of empty slope between widely spaced shrubs. This editable meadow study adds a much lower layer that *belongs to this ground*: four dimorphic, wind-combed fans in twelve broken swaths along the actual sheltered grade. Copper dry blades turn through folded olive leaves. They grow in 131 discontinuous rhizomes, rather than a regularly planted row or a radial grass brush. All 590 shoot coordinates and their true native planting-plane gradients are archived; old shrubs retain their places, and no new food, cover, walkability, collision, physics, save or default is defined.

The near scene uses 122 folded shoots and 468 simpler shoots (60,984 placed triangles). The separate guardian state switches all 590 shoots to four 4–6 triangle shapes (2,936 placed triangles). These are mutually exclusive, static artist LOD choices, not an engine benchmark. A grounded 18.75 × 12.5 m section of the slope receives a 1.95 cm/texel pigment transfer: traces of fallen ochre blades and dark organic seams respond to the actual grass pockets without hiding the original substrate. The plant-only files are independently reusable: no original land, water or tree meshes are duplicated inside them.

There are **952 real V1 surface triangles**, plus 180 vertical 20 m streaming-skirt faces in the archived 1,132-face native *render primitive*. Those skirts previously showed up as strips dangling beneath the open-sided model. This artist desk removes only those drawing aids, and clips its own sea sheet against the actual submerged native triangles. No native chunk, vertex, coast or tree was changed. The unchanged older desk is preserved at fda268b2.

**Opening the edit.** The four glTF scenes are held Hollow, held Rise, proposed close meadow, proposed guardian meadow. For a direct A/B use scene 1 before; scene 2 after for the two body-height views; scene 3 after for the guardian. Camera 0 looks uphill at the actual twin crowns from the original 1.04 m eye, camera 1 is the 129 m orthographic section, and camera 2 looks toward the nearest existing willow from the very same eye. Colour desk illustrations use the actual GLB triangles/instances, linear filtered UVs, original crown alpha cutoff and a simple matte directional light. Their grey surround is outside the crop. No sky, shadow, native timing, gameplay frame or Blender execution is implied.

**Author.** `plan.py NATIVE HOLLOW RISE OUTPUT` writes the registered plants. `kit.py EARLIER-TWIN-NEEDLE.glb OUTPUT` exports the four-scene edit, the mutually exclusive eye and guardian plant-only GLBs and a neutral-edge thatch transfer. `material.py` is the optional Blender-only binder for the existing full habitat setup: apply after the held Rise ground coat and load exactly one plant-only file. It is authoring source, not a renderer registration.
