# The same 1.04 m eye

Left (glance-1.png) shows the held windwillow and original rise; right (glance-2.png) changes only the registered optical soil treatment, original-ground stitchgrass and its small debris. Both use the same local CPU shadow reconstruction from their real GLB triangles; this is a field assembly view, not Blender or a game frame.

Visible result: the near turf no longer reads as one bare olive surface under the willow; several attached grass fans carry upright amber scales, spent blade/hull shapes break the floor, and a warmer organic bed reaches into the shelter. The middle and near picture share their existing spruce and terrain. Source-mesh shadows are filtered, not the rejected block-edged first draw. The portable soil still looks softer and flatter than a full native material. Do not use this as proof that the island meets a shipping visual or performance target.

The 455,208-byte local specimen palette and 771-root spatial register remain unchanged. It takes one mesh per species/form at distance. The studio retains the original 952 physical-surface triangles in the cut; 180 native rendering-skirt faces are not part of the physical surface. No native game, tests, CI, PC, terrain, save or brain modification.
