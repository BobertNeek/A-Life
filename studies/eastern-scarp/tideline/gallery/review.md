# Gullglass: at the tide pockets

The archived 29mm shore comparison adds small, uneven chalk-worn pockets to the otherwise smooth toe of the cliff. At the 33mm footing angle, the nearest form has a visibly rolled split rim and dark opening; farther casts are just small marks on the ground. No exaggeration of the effect: the large blue-grey backlit rock face and empty middle distance remain. The four new upper-face exposures are deliberately excluded from both sides.
