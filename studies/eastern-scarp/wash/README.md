# Gullglass: the outer wash

A separate shoreline-optics proposal, not part of the headland or tide-pocket comparisons. The current held sea retains the native albedo lookup and 58 m normal tile, but reads like a single uninterrupted reflector next to a ninety-odd-metre cut. The proposed optical wash is only a small addition on truly submerged original points. No new sea, raising/lowering water or fake offshore beach.

survey.py runs against the held native V1 height source. It finds the outermost falling crossing at sea level and at four real depths, records original world-space edge and the local 6 m prominence. Only use these measured widths, inclined local wave directions and disconnected lobes to design partial broken swash, not a bright visible border around the whole island. Keep substantial clean water between groups. A later after cannot be described as native water shading: the original maps remain underneath, but any optical layer is expressly an artist proposal.
