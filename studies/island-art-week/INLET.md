# Southern inlet: read the terrain before painting it

The wide east cove at (345,117) is a sandy, gently graded margin, not a cliff.
Do not manufacture a wall there to make a screenshot dramatic. The actual V1
southern outlet is different: at (84,419) ground is about 4.2 m high with
local gradient (-1.32,+0.54); only 5 m east it is below water. Across the
visible channel at (112,428) the wall rises the other way. They are opposing
banks of the existing roughly 20 m wide tidal channel, **not open-sea bluffs**.
Nearby upper ground is already about 9–10 m high. This is a good place for
a materially richer creature-height reach without moving any simulation water.

Start from two genuine photographs: along the west shoulder looking across the
inlet and an upstream oblique guardian view showing the two banks joining the
outer shore. Preserve the recovered fern, alder and selected sea alongside the
terrain. If the unmodified still does not actually show a wall, choose a better
real location before drawing. Paint damp slate and compact reddish silt close
to water, brighter weathering just below the turf lip, drying fissures and a
few roots escaping from the real soil. Follow the steep cell faces, local
runoff and breaks in cover. Do not lay bright strata at identical water heights,
stamp a periodic noise atlas from one end to the other, or run a green hedge
along both sides. Keep the flat sandy mouth and already sunlit meadow quiet.

Prefer a region-specific alpha-edged surface fitted to the existing triangles
or a genuinely localized material blend. The close/far transition must not
flash, and no triangle may change navigable ground, the wet channel, old saves,
the optional ridge, or a user's willow crossing. If V1 and V2 differ in the
pilot area, omit those cells from any shared overlay. Preserve source masks,
PBR artwork and absolute cameras. Next week, when testing is allowed, check the
bank on foot/picking, water contact, deck occlusion and stability while rolling
a toy. For now only Blender artwork, source and asset authoring.

There is one real BoulderC (placement 1460 around x85.4,z416.9) in the wall
and two original scree objects in this reach. Trees are up on the safer
shoulders, not planted in both banks. Do not fake giant exposed tree roots on
the bare slope. Any grass fibres should hang from small discontinuous groups
on the *actual* change of grade; compare remaining undisturbed sections.
The first unique painted chart traces thirteen local runoff starts on the
unmodified V1 triangles, with damp hollows, quiet iron staining and embedded
river-sized clasts. It has not yet been accepted in the actual landscape.
