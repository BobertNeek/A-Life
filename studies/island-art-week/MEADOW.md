# Starting-valley grassland: draw the whole community at eye height

Pick this up **after** settling the separate southern-inlet study. Grass is the
single most common stock island prop (1,019); 234 flower clumps share its old
leaf recipe. The October source gives every tuft the same 24 spokes and every
flower clump six flat radial petals on seven upright stems. Remodelling only the
woodland fern did not replace this open-country grass carpet. Preserve the
already authored nursery ribbon-grass and moss; their native 25 m garden around
81.25,250 is not this work.

The original lower valley contains an exceptionally good existing meadow:
around grass placement 3406 (74.75,301.40), 20 original grass placements fall
within 10 m, nine Flowers within 15 m, no overstory tree in the same 15 m.
Several dozen original trees rise behind the western/northwestern side, and a
real rock lies toward 83.3,278.4. Photograph it first from 1.0 m looking
through the seeded grassland, and from a nearby raised guardian composition
which shows quiet space between communities. Do not use a rearranged terrarium
in place of those real shots.

If the baseline confirms upright spike tufts and cutout daisies, aim for
windcombed dune/riverside sedge, lower glaucous folded blades in shelter, and
an occasional tan seedhead rather than a seed stalk on every copy. For the
flowering community use restrained soft-sci-fi cup-and-lantern bellforms, not
floating fluorescent beads. Broad green masses come from actual coherent
patches with visible bare slots, not isolated identical plants on a busy
meadow texture. Work to a near/guardian pair and a whole-scene composition;
hold any garden that turns into carpet noise. Preserve placement transforms,
collision/bounds, original IDs, all simulation/saves and nursery plant assets.
Prefer opaque shaded geometry or one compact, justified cutout family; no extra
draw loops simply to vary a flower colour. Save an editable set and static LOD
counts. No game, performance, tests, CI tests or user PC this week.
