# Continuing the real lower valley

The old starter-valley Flowers’ five-pointed paper-looking cups and stiff basal leaves still show at original 3394. The guarded **authoring option** `wax_petals: true` draws curved petal-lobes, a rolled rim, visible cream pollen in the hollow throat, small overlapping sepals at the neck, and a ribbon of daylight on a more naturally drooping basal leaf. It does not change the selected native flower. The ordinary generator still reproduces the installed a50dca41 asset until a future artist selects the new one. From the existing original Grass and Flowers libraries, use `studio_meadow_flowers.py OUT --author-from SOURCES --wax-petals` inside Blender to author it; the saved editable `.blend` exports without regeneration. Use the already documented meadow-bells camera before deciding. Preserve the terrain and all existing plant positions.
