# Island art handoff: which version a photograph shows

1. V1 is the actual original game terrain. The selected shore/water, exposed rock,
   spruce, alder and fern are art changes only. A new mesh or material on the
   weekly branch does not by itself mean an in-game capture.
2. The separate V2 highland prototype was generated from its own versioned
   physical heightfield for the earlier northern-ridge experiment. A V1 meadow
   photo intentionally cannot establish that those V2 mountain shapes have
   been installed. Keep the two labels independent.
3. The southern bend is a third kind of thing: a DCC-only shape study with an
   editable .blend and no native terrain ID. Neither old saves nor new worlds
   see its channel. Study gallery 507a2cbd includes the upstream foreground
   defect rather than cropping it out.
4. The separate village lane builds on the actual nursery. Its cultivated
   meadow near x64,z251 is not the authored wild valley around x75,z301. The
   artists can reuse original approved pine/alder/fern products for offscreen
   stills without merging branches or replacing layout.
5. Native renderer and target GPU evaluation remain for next week. Marks
   against screenshots are art review evidence, not gameplay, frame-time, or
   collision evidence. Do not start a test simply because the artist finished.
