# Young saltwood on the existing wild valley

[Matched habitat at original #1116](young-saltwood.jpg) · [same asset on the artist’s workbench](young-saltwood-workbench.jpg). The original artistic silhouette was a bundle of smooth ellipsoids with disconnected leaf flecks. The revised 4 m tree keeps its actual original wood at all three levels, grows branched leaders from all fourteen original woody fork ends and the leader, and seats a few lower shoots directly on those stems. Each broad, folded, entirely opaque leaf tapers at the petiole and tip; a few new shoots flush warm bronze. At 100 m the grouped shoots retain the directions of surviving near leaves; at 240 m small connecting woody arms meet irregular tapered boughs. [Same-scale sculpting sheet for all three native zoom levels](young-saltwood-levels.jpg), not an engine transition test. All 164 original placements, identities, elevations, yaw and scale remain the same.

Original triangle budgets 3124, 672, 56; current 5732, 612, 180. The fully modeled close asset in the habitat still is unchanged; the middle/far forms were subsequently drawn down from 2,124/268 triangles, with connected distant boughs.
