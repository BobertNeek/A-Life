# Grounding the glacial deposit: weekly art, not a gameplay checkpoint

The original V1 woodland contains 123 BoulderA, 148 BoulderB, 153 BoulderC. Each includes *three* original rock bodies. The raw L0/L1/overview of each is 312/140/62 triangles. The 62-triangle original far meshes, UVs and original PBR material are held at far range. Near and middle now have 442/208, 442/216 and 456/212 triangles, one primitive per LOD. The whole released A/B/C object AABBs, and each physical component’s six extent values, retain their native values and all placements stay fixed. The new sole rounds **inward** below the bottom 46% instead of stretching down into collision-free air. It does not retain the whole outline of the original flat sole, nor does it make a steep illegal stand physically safe.

At original 1570, the old full lower face ranged 0.41 m inside the real uphill slope to 0.58 m above the downhill grade; at 1383, from -0.40 m to +0.31 m. Across all 424 original lower outline samples, the median worst downhill clearance is 0.177 m; 87 exceed 0.50 m, 32 exceed 1 m and the greatest is 6.27 m. This is a static geometric source observation, not a physical simulation. A second inclined rock site cannot support a literal uniform horizontal stone floor. A slimmer lower skin can imply local contact; never fill an unoccupied volume under the saved opaque box. If site-specific re-siting or raised fill is judged necessary next week, that belongs to a separately versioned physical change, never this material swap.

The stone alone kept a prominent grey dome under flat light. The photographed close finish gives an actual stone a truncated glacial facet, crystalline/iron layers and discontinuous pale weather rind on upfaces. The response is not a warm colour grade or bright moss blanket. Original overviews, original cliff/scree material, forest and sky never received that shader.

The 3-site local till proposal adds partially buried 2–14 cm long, at most 5 cm tall fragments and folded woodrush on the actual native triangle surface, thin in the down-slope direction. It is a geological habitat patch, not a fake shadow or elevated foundation. It covers only unchanged sites 1570, 1383 and 1675. Do not claim all 424 have acquired that scenery. For any future native tile overlay, key by actual original V1 surface/landmark fingerprint. Drop it if a player changes those terrain triangles; never establish another navigation surface. Three whole tile totals: 2,761/2,878/2,683 triangles, cull at near range; distant hills require no new cards.
