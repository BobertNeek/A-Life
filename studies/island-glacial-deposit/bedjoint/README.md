### What the correction is allowed to do

BoulderA #1383 is three existing stone bodies and one real saved obstacle. Its near camera is the same 1.04 m `talus-eye` that exposed the dark gap. The mount finds where each original projected stone overlaps the *rising* real terrain above the original obstacle floor, then keys a broader low heel into this material. It does not raise the ground or draw a plinth in the air below the box. Preserve the upper shoulders and shape the transition within each old horizontal projection.
