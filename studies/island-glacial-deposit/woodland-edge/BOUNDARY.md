# Where this offline study stops

The matched images and the editable cut demonstrate one authored ordinary V1 landmark, not an installed renderer, all 134 locations, or a play session. Directly renaming ScreeSeat1303.glb to Scree.glb would mis-seat the other 133 original deposits. The separate rock material work for Boulder A/B/C is also not included in the game. The large adjacent solid boulder still has its own old unsupported downhill silhouette; neither the contact fix nor a forest bed is meant to disguise it.

If selected *after* next week's native review, keep immutable source-landmark identity and near-only visual dressing. A practical broader option is to reuse the original 22 closed stone bodies per LOD. When an actual visible neighborhood enters memory, compose them once from its local transforms and the real current world triangles. Invalidate/reseat only a touched deposit when a player reshapes that terrain, not every frame or every save. Keep one composed render primitive/material per active deposit rather than 22 per-stone draw calls. A bounded cache or a shared buffer can be considered; no size, handoff, frame cost or reliability claim has been measured here. Beyond the authored near range, retain the cheap stock behavior unless the change of silhouette is itself visible.

For an older save or unsupported geography use its genuine terrain and ordinary plant state; don't interpret “V1” as permission to overwrite an edited valley. Decorative stones make no collision promise; solid boulders/Cliffs are separate physical identities. The woodland art is local too: the actual two spruce at 111/131, fern 2510 and old two-sided wash can't be broadcast over a different grove. A combined near patch should disappear or be recomposed if its soil changes. Consider the rightmost foreground grass and the old distant highland columns before making the woodland more widespread; a field of identical stamps won't help.
