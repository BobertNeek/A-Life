# A softer mouth: a Blender sculpt, not game terrain

The completed V1 bank material can show damp clay, suspended gravel pockets
and a turf edge. At creature height it is still a giant plane; from overhead
the two waterlines track almost in parallel through the entire last reach. A
different colour cannot change either fact. This experiment changes **shape**.

The reference and proposal use the same sun, water composition, trees, stones
and bank art, and the same two original cameras. The alternative takes a short
bite out of one outer bank and leaves a tapered, low point bar opposite it.
An earlier bend turns back in the other direction. The physical river above the
study, outer coastal footprint, water level, camera positions and surrounding
landmarks are fixed. These are estimated forms, not hydraulic simulation.
Vegetation and props move with their local studied bank only. The approved
water's composition stays the same; its world-aligned optical chart is carried
into the bend so that the old channel is not ghosted under the new shore.

This directory is outside the game's assets and crates. There is no terrain
version, packaged collider, import into the renderer, save binding or game
toggle here. It proposes no edits to player causeways. An actual adoption would
require its own separately authorized versioned world design and compatibility
review; the correct result of reviewing this art can simply be “keep V1.”

The editable local model has the real triangulated V1 basis, fitted V1 bank
art and three artist sliders (bend, point bar, outer scour). All zero returns
to the original local terrain and all one is the proposal. The neutral working
clay makes the shape easy to sculpt. The photographs instead use all the
scene's original habitat textures, placed trees, coastal lighting and water.

Reproduce the photographed study with the repository's Blender artwork producer
and the inlet-concept board. No native process, world fixture or tests needed.
