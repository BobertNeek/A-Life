# Beneath the spruce

At the new 58-metre guardian angle the left exposed slope originally breaks into conspicuous pointed polygon roofs. In the final right photograph those large hard faces are gone. Broad cool mineral colour and irregular warm sediment remain on the actual slope with fine clasts still legible; the trees, old V1 terrain contour and lighting have not moved. The 1.04-metre camera retains the foreground windcups and their dispersed chaff. Its new middle-distance brush is modest rather than a new forest; the sea was a constant simple studio reference in both photographs, not native ocean footage. The artist selection plate identifies the former roof surfaces in red and small apron pieces in gold. True landscape and static collision are unmodified.

Blender only, with exact real V1 angles and positions. No native mesh binding, native frame timings, gameplay or change to physics, saves, brains, workflows or default.
