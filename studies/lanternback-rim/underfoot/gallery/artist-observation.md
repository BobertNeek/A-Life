# Pennon Drift from the two held eyes and 143 m guardian

At the same eye, the previously empty ground now catches copper seed husks, thin bleached sheaths and intermittent slate in separate downslope deposits. The rooted clump and the clear space uphill stay readable. The added detail is apparent near the animal, not an island-wide change: the guardian comparison retains the earlier bedrock shapes, and draws no individual loose pieces. Some of the close mineral facets remain sharply angular, and the held triangular flower heads are still crude; their morphology is a separate design, not a result of changing the soil. The two matched creature positions draw 10,823 and 10,644 loose-detail triangles respectively in this offline setup; no runtime cost was measured.
