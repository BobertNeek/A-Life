# If the art is selected for native staging next week

This folder is not an installed game feature. The existing weekly source commit, Blender edit and photos document a proposal. The following cut is deliberately smaller than "make a new ecosystem."

1. Work in an opt-in visuals-only checkout and a disposable V1 profile. Resolve the installed map by its existing V1 identity, not by a map display name. Its exported frozen height source is SHA-256 8d0e9cf6343ffe8ea5e82bcb7ad3e4ef77eef7207042cf0274de5d91f2457c38; any procedural/edited overlap needs explicit handling. Never restore these small visual overlays into a save or create an obstacle, item, tile, gameplay plant, brain input or reserved navigation cell.
2. Treat the original triangle heights as authority. Most grit is only 1–14 cm long. Its locations belong in metres, in engine +X/+Y-up/+Z, with glTF node translations retained, not local meshes dropped at the world origin. X runs east, Z increases south. Do not apply a second Blender y/z conversion after asset import. No mesh replaces the terrain or a solid prop.
3. The art chart covers x 234 to 264 and z −165 to −134. It clips outside that box; edge pixels are transparent, never extend the border. Its RGB is sRGB, its alpha linear, and soil-tooth is linear data. Under the native ground shader, amount equals chart alpha; don't blacken adjacent rock or add depth/displacement. Use the earlier broader Lanternback material first, then the near-only soil paint. A normal derived from the shallow height detail, not physically raised soil, is the intended result. Do not apply either material to rocks or leaves just because they share a chunk.
4. Each L0/L1 pair has the same five-metre horizontal cell and constant true-world anchors, as listed in cells.json. For geometry selection use point-to-three-dimensional-AABB distance to the NEAR bounds for both forms. The offline proof uses near out to 6.75 m, then middle through 16 m, then neither. Native camera height/orthographic span/envelope and a short hysteresis or fade need deliberation in motion; stationary Blender plates cannot establish a clean transition. At the 143 m guardian frame use no individual centimetre-scale fragments. Root-level decorative GLBs must not each add an entire duplicate parent terrain.

Observe whether unopened soil still reads as open earth, the deposit weights fade without an outline, the shoreside face is not submerged and the loose matter does not hover. Inspect the full hill from the guardian and walk/roll a real toy in the throwaway profile. A render, native binding check or vertex ledger alone cannot establish toy contact, save safety in production or RTX performance.
