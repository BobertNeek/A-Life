[Four matched full-island photographs](gallery/Shellback-Reach.jpg) · [Editable studio recipe](desk.py). This is an isolated visual study, not a native install.

From above, low vegetation now collects in uneven wind-sheltered groups between the old erratic and the headland trees. It leaves a legible open passage, rather than planting both sides of a line. From a 1.04 m eye, the hummocky meadow changes into mineral ground below the original bluff alder and then into open sand with occasional casts, weathered siltstone and a few exposed roots. The old straight inlet, distant mountains and existing tree flares have not magically been reworked. The largest visible change is around the bluff and shore; the wider meadow change is more restrained.

## The crossing

The 218.88 m surveyed dry line starts outside the cultivated ground at (80.7, 266.4), passes the genuine wild sedge #3406 and the old ice stone #1364, rises through the headland with saved alders #1067, #213 and #559 and finishes on the actual southern beach at (133.8, 466.2). The form and the waterline are unmodified. The hollow, sunlight and old solid landmarks seen in the reference are not reclaimed as new work.

The line crosses ordinary V1 triangles from **0.648 to 10.37 m above water**. Its steepest crossed triangle is **0.4912 rise/run**, versus the ordinary body’s 0.85. Conservative minimum clearance along the full segments is **3.159 m** to any saved obstacle; ordinary body radius is 0.20 m. Distances are based on exact X/Z cell and diagonal split points and expanded AABBs, not an in-game traversal or a claim about a particular creature. [321 surveyed stations](gallery/stations.csv) and [actual native anchor transforms](gallery/route.json). V1 height bytes, obstacle boxes, all original placements, the selected default and every save are unchanged. No new gameplay path, item, plant state or collider was registered.

## How the picture is made

Both sides use ordinary terrain LOD, existing close habitats and meadow, inlet, native water and canopy, original transforms, actual directional light and identical lens/exposure. The right adds a world-aligned earth/shell wash to the original triangles, tiny masked normal relief and separately editable source-only detail. The windy wild margin opens around till lenses; the last sandy bluff carries greener rolled leaves, rusty-tipped succulent fans and occasional half-buried five-rib valves. They are visual centimetre-scale shore dressing, not a row of new solid rocks. The rooted scrub on the sheltered shoulders is a separate [editable study mesh](gallery/reach-scrub.glb); the [smaller surface and grass forms](gallery/reach-detail.glb) remain independently editable. The [buried alder root and siltstone](gallery/bluff-foot.glb) and the [centimetre sediment chart](gallery/bluff-grain.png) are restrained to a 50 m radius of the real original tree #559. They have no effect in either of the distant frozen after views. Larger island geo including the previous boulder/woodland study is left alone. 303,804 total isolated authoring triangles over the complete crossing, divided into metre-addressed sections; no hardware-cost claim. The editing desk holds actual V1 surface triangles, the native macro-texture cut, current unmodified native witness-tree/stone meshes, actual guide stations, geometry and the matched gallery. Its local cut is intentionally not the full-island lit photography scene.
